Material sourcing — exactly which brands we use, and why

Every component in a WoodenMax window is from a named, traceable, mill-certified vendor. No "no-name imports from Tier-3 cities". Here is the active vendor matrix.

  • Aluminium: Jindal, Hindalco — both BIS-licensed Indian mills
  • Glass: Saint-Gobain, Asahi India, Modi Guard — top-3 in India
  • Hardware: SIEGENIA / ROTO / GU / HOPPE — German imports
  • Powder: PPG Envirocron / Akzo Nobel Interpon — Qualicoat-approved

Aluminium — the body

Why we don't spec "no-name local hardware"
Hardware is <5% of system cost but >60% of long-term complaints. A rusted roller can ruin a ₹1.2 L sliding door. Spending an extra ₹450 on a SIEGENIA roller is the single highest-leverage decision in the entire window stack.

Vendor audit — how we keep this honest

Each approved vendor is on a quarterly or annual on-site audit cycle conducted by our engineering team. The audit covers:

  • Raw-material certificates and lot traceability
  • Process consistency vs. last audit
  • QC defect rate trend
  • On-time delivery and order accuracy
  • Sustainability — recycled content, packaging, water/energy use

Vendors who fail two consecutive audits move to "Watch" status. Three failures → de-listed. We have removed two suppliers from the approved list in the last 5 years for exactly this reason.

Frequently asked questions

Do you ever use unbranded hardware to hit a tight budget?

On the cost-optimised tier we substitute SIEGENIA/ROTO with Domus or Mahaveer Indian-branded hardware — not unbranded. These come with 2-year warranty and meet our 10,000-cycle minimum. We never use Tier-3 imports without warranty.

Can I specify which brand of hardware goes on my windows?

Yes. The quote breakdown lists default hardware. You can upgrade to SIEGENIA/GU on a per-opening basis and we will re-quote in 24 hours.

How do I verify the aluminium grade after install?

Each profile carries a faint ink-jet code on the inner face during extrusion. Send us a photo of the code and we share back the mill test certificate within 24 hours.

Is the glass really branded Saint-Gobain on my project?

Yes. Every DGU has the Saint-Gobain spacer logo etched on the spacer bar inside the unit. After install you can see it clearly at the perimeter of the glass.

Do you have a vendor list I can pull into my Excel for vendor empanelment?

Email compliance@woodenmax.in for a signed copy of our approved-vendor list. We share within 48 hours.
