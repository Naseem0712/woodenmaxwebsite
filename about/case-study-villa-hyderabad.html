Project brief

A 9,400 sqft built-up area villa on a 1,000 sqyd plot. Architect from Hyderabad, interiors by a Mumbai studio. The brief: frameless visual continuity from inside to outside, fully home-automation integrated, and acoustically calm despite the busy Banjara Hills road location.

Wind-load exposure: low-to-moderate (Hyderabad inland). Sun exposure: south-west primary facade gets 6 hours of harsh afternoon sun. Acoustic load: 65–72 dB at the road-facing facade.

How we ran the project (family + partner model)

At any given time WoodenMax is running 10–12 live projects across India in parallel. We deliberately keep volumes capped to this band — it is the maximum number our family + trained partner-crew model can ship without quality drift.

For this villa: design + fabrication + QC was 100% in-house at our Hyderabad factory. On-site install was led by our senior site-supervisor, with execution by a 4-person partner crew that has been with us since 2020. The owner had a single WhatsApp group with the WoodenMax brother in charge of the project — calls returned within an hour, decisions taken on call, no "talk to my manager" handoffs.

What went well

  • Architect, builder, owner aligned on system spec early — no scope creep mid-production
  • Single-crew install kept work disruption to a defined window for the family
  • Pre-shipping factory visit by the owner caught a colour-tone preference change before powder-coat run
  • The motorised pergola was delivered + commissioned 4 days before the housewarming as agreed

What we learned for next time

Two small lessons
On day 6 of install, we discovered the home-automation cabling for the pergola needed a different conduit path than originally drawn. We adjusted on site but next time we will ask for an automation drawing review at design freeze. Second — the owner wanted late-stage colour-change on the inner gasket (black to grey). We could do it but it added 2 days to ship gaskets. Future order checklists will include gasket-colour at design freeze.

How was the structural-glazing facade attached without a visible frame?

The DGU units are bonded to an aluminium sub-frame using Dow Corning DC-993 structural silicone, with a 4-side mechanical safety capture on the inside. The visible joint is a 12 mm weather silicone bead between adjacent units. Wind-load qualified at +2.0 kPa.
