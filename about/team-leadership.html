How we hire

Three principles, written on the wall in our HR room:

  1. Hire on character first — anyone with a "the customer was wrong" attitude is filtered out at the second round, regardless of skill.
  2. Hire for 5-year tenure — every offer we make assumes the candidate will be with us in 2031. We invest accordingly.
  3. Promote first, recruit second — 4 of our 5 department heads were internal promotions, not lateral hires.

Frequently asked questions

Can I meet the engineer designing my project?

Yes. For projects > ₹5 L, the assigned design engineer accompanies the site visit. For B2B/architect projects, we set up a 30-min design-kickoff call after the first site visit.

Who do I escalate to if my project is going wrong?

First — your account manager. If unresolved in 48 hours — write to info@woodenmax.com with subject "Escalation — <Quote No>". The founder personally reads every escalation within 24 hours.

Are your installation crews on payroll?

Service engineers and supervisors are W2. Installation crews (4–6 people per crew) are independent contractors under CLRA-licensed agreements, with workmen's compensation, ESI and PF cover routed via us. They have been with us 4–9 years on average.

Do you hire?

Always — engineering, production, and field service. Send a CV to info@woodenmax.com (subject "Careers"). We respond to every CV within 7 days even when there is no open role.
