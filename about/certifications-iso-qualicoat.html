What Qualicoat Class 2 actually means

Qualicoat is the international quality label for liquid and powder coatings on aluminium. Class 1 is suitable for standard inland exposure; Class 2 upgrades pre-treatment chemistry & uses a super-durable polyester resin — required for tropical & coastal-adjacent India. We default to Class 2 on every powder-coated profile.

The Qualicoat label requires the licence-holder to:

  • Use only approved powder suppliers (PPG, Akzo Nobel, Jotun)
  • Maintain the 5-stage pre-treatment with chrome-free passivation
  • Coat thickness 60–100 microns (we run 60–80 for architectural aesthetic)
  • Pass quarterly third-party audits with cross-cut, impact, cupping, and salt-spray tests

What ISO 9001:2015 actually means

ISO 9001 is the global QMS framework. For a window fabricator, the audit covers:

  • Documented quality manual + SOPs for every production stage
  • Calibration register for measurement tools (saw, gauges, torque wrenches)
  • Supplier qualification & vendor scorecards
  • Internal-audit log + management-review minutes
  • Non-conformance + corrective-action records
  • Customer-complaint log + closure timeline

The certification body audits annually + does an unannounced surveillance visit. Our 2025 audit closed with zero major non-conformities.

How to verify these certifications independently

  • GST: Use the official GST search portal — type our GSTIN to see active status, address, and registered scope
  • ISO 9001: Look up our certificate number on the TUV NORD India database
  • Qualicoat: Check the global Qualicoat licensee directory at qualicoat.net
  • MSME / Udyam: Search our Udyam number on the official MSME Udyam Registration portal
  • Test reports: NABL labs (NTH, ICOMM) maintain digital report verification — quote the report number on their portal

Frequently asked questions

Where can I download soft copies of your certifications?

Email compliance@woodenmax.in with your company name and intended use. We share within 48 hours under NDA for B2B; for individual customers we share at the time of order signing.

Are these certifications mandatory for my project?

For a residential home, no — most municipalities don't require them. For a commercial high-rise sanction, fire NOC, or for builders going to RERA, they significantly de-risk approval. For NHAI / CPWD / Railway tenders they are essential.

What is the difference between BIS and ISI?

BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) writes the standards. ISI is the older mark of conformance to a specific BIS standard. Our aluminium is sourced from BIS-licensed mills (Jindal Aluminium, Hindalco) under IS 733 alloy spec.

Are your installation crews insured?

Yes. Workmen's Compensation Insurance via Bajaj Allianz covers every installation worker. Public Liability Insurance covers third-party damage during installation. Schedule copy on request.
