Brief — a vegetarian cafe with two distinct moods

Makobrew is a vegetarian restaurant & cafe brand opening twin outlets in Hyderabad. The brief was to give each outlet a distinct architectural mood while keeping the brand DNA — clean lines, warm wood ceilings, expansive glass — instantly recognisable across both.

Himayat Nagar was designed as the flagship — full luxury glass elevation on the street face, a roof-level retractable pergola for the al-fresco section, and a sweeping fluted-wood ceiling that ties the entire floorplate together.

Jubilee Hills was designed as the boutique sister outlet — a more intimate footprint with fabric ceiling, premium glass partitions defining zones, and a custom passage-light installation along the entry corridor.

Why the fluted-wood ceiling drove the project
At 5,000 sft the ceiling is the single largest visual element of the space. We pre-fabricated panels in 1.2 m × 2.4 m modules at our Hyderabad factory, pre-finished, and assembled on site in 9 working days. This compressed what a stick-built ceiling would have taken 3+ weeks to do.

The 10–12 live projects model — and why it matters

At any given time WoodenMax is running 10–12 live projects across India in parallel. We deliberately cap volumes at this band — it is the maximum a family-led + trained-partner-crew model can ship without quality drift. Makobrew was one of 11 live projects during its build window; concurrent projects included two villas in Hyderabad, a builder development in Pune, and the Mumbai commercial tower (see other case studies).

For the client, this model meant: (1) a single WhatsApp group with the WoodenMax brother running the project, (2) decisions taken on call without a "talk to my manager" handoff, and (3) the same team that quoted is the team that delivered.

What went well

  • Parallel build of both outlets compressed the launch by about 6 weeks vs. sequential delivery
  • Pre-fabricated fluted-wood ceiling modules turned the slowest element of the spec into the fastest
  • Retractable pergola was rain-sensor-tested before commissioning — operator handover was a 30-minute walk-through, not a 2-hour training
  • Custom chandeliers were finalised in 2 sketch iterations because the same WoodenMax engineer who quoted was on the design calls

What we learned

Two small lessons
On the fabric ceiling at Jubilee Hills, we initially specified a higher-tension fabric that proved acoustically harsh in test. We swapped to a softer 280 gsm fabric mid-build — lost 4 days but the operator's post-install feedback validated the change. Second — the HPL back-of-house elevation needed an extra moisture-barrier behind the kitchen wall that wasn't in the original drawing. We added it on site. Future drawings include kitchen-wall moisture-barrier as default.
