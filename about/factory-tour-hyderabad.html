The 7 stations a profile travels through

1

Material receipt & lot logging

Every aluminium billet enters with a mill test certificate. Alloy grade, temper, and lot number are logged before the billet enters the cutting bay.

2

Precision cutting

Twin-head Italian mitre saws cut to ±0.1 mm. Every cut is logged against the shop drawing for your opening — no shared inventory.

3

CNC machining

Drainage slots, lock pockets, drainage caps, weep holes — all routed on a 3-axis CNC. The profile leaves the bay ready for assembly.

4

Powder coating

Qualicoat Class-2 booth — degrease, chrome-free passivation, electrostatic powder spray (PPG / Akzo Nobel), 12-min cure at 200°C. Coat thickness verified at 60–80 microns on every shutter.

5

Glazing line

DGU units arrive sealed from Saint-Gobain / Asahi / Modi with their warranty papers. Our glazier fits them into the profile with EPDM-cushion gaskets — never raw silicone-on-glass.

6

Quality control

Each finished shutter is tested for: smooth operation (10 cycles), corner-joint integrity (4 mm gauge), water-bead test with a 50 mm Schauberger nozzle, and final visual.

7

Pack & dispatch

Each opening is foam-wrapped, corner-protected, labelled by opening number, and crated by site. Crates leave with your name, project address and a barcode that ties back to the shop drawing.

How "QC pass = ready to dispatch" works
A finished shutter cannot move from the QC bay to the packing bay without a green sticker carrying the QC supervisor's initials and date. The packing-bay supervisor refuses any shutter without that sticker. This single-sticker control is the simplest reason WoodenMax field-return rate sits at 0.4%.

Frequently asked questions

Is the factory really yours, or do you outsource?

Fully in-house. The machinery and core team are 100% WoodenMax — operated by the founding family. At any time we run 10–12 live projects across India in parallel; site execution is shared with our trained partner crews under direct WoodenMax site-management.

Why does single-facility, single-team matter?

Because every joint, gasket, coating decision is taken by the same engineers. There is no inter-vendor blame game when an issue arises — service requests land back at the same factory that built the unit.

Do you serve cities outside Hyderabad?

Yes. All production happens in Hyderabad; finished crates ship by road to 14 cities. Free transport applies on orders ≥ ₹15 L within 1,000 km from Hyderabad — see policy.

Can I bring a structural engineer with me on the tour?

Of course. We routinely host architects and structural engineers. We will line up the relevant test data, system drawings and reference projects for your engineer to review.

Is there a video tour?

We are producing a professional 4-minute walk-through scheduled to launch in Q3 2026. Until then, a live virtual tour (Google Meet, 30 minutes, dedicated guide) can be booked any working day.
