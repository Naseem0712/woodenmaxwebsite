## FAQ — Aluminium Sliding Windows

**Q1. Aluminium sliding window aur uPVC window mein kya fark hai?**
Aluminium 3x zyada strong hai, slim frames deta hai, aur India ke heat mein warp nahi karta. uPVC thoda better insulation deta hai lekin large openings mein sag jaata hai. Budget: aluminium 27mm Domal ₹550–950/sqft, uPVC ₹700–1100/sqft. Indian climate ke liye aluminium better choice hai.

**Q2. 2-track aur 3-track sliding window mein kya difference hai?**
2-track = 2 glass panels, koi mesh nahi. Maximum glass area milta hai, clean look. 3-track = 2 glass panels + 1 sliding mesh panel. Ventilation ke saath insect protection milta hai. Bedrooms aur balconies ke liye 3-track recommend karte hain, large living room windows ke liye 2-track.

**Q3. 27mm Domal aur 29mm premium series mein kya fark hai?**
27mm Domal: wall thickness 1–1.2mm, price ₹550–950/sqft, max height 6ft recommended. Standard residential use ke liye. 29mm Premium: wall thickness 1.2mm (uniform sab sections mein), slim interlock mechanism, price ₹1,200–1,400/sqft, 80kg panel capacity. Premium homes ke liye.

**Q4. DGU glass kya hota hai aur kab choose karein?**
DGU = Double Glazed Unit — do glass ke beech mein sealed air/argon gap. Ye heat 40% tak reduce karta hai aur noise 35dB tak kam karta hai. AC rooms, road-facing windows, aur premium villas ke liye ideal. Extra cost: ₹180–220/sqft. Agar bijli bill zyada hai ya traffic noise problem hai — DGU lein.

**Q5. Aluminium sliding window kitne saal chalti hai?**
6063 T6 grade aluminium frames 20–25 saal aaram se chalte hain. Powder coating 10–15 saal. Imported rollers/hardware 8–10 saal smooth rehte hain. Annual maintenance sirf track cleaning aur roller lubrication — koi painting nahi, koi rust nahi.

**Q6. Maximum kitni height tak sliding window safe hai?**
27mm Domal series ke liye max recommended height 6ft hai. 6–8ft tak ja sakte hain but vibration/shake ho sakti hai. 29mm premium series 8ft tak stable rehti hai. 8ft+ ke liye premium series ya system window recommend karte hain.

**Q7. Mesh baad mein add ho sakti hai ya pehle hi leni chahiye?**
3-track window mein mesh integrated hoti hai — track already built-in hota hai. Baad mein separate clip-on mesh add ho sakti hai lekin woh utni smooth nahi hoti. Agar mesh chahiye toh order ke time hi specify karein.

**Q8. Powder coating kitne colors mein milti hai?**
Standard: white, off-white, black, grey, brown — sabmein included. Wooden grain finish (teak, rosewood, oak look) ₹50/sqft extra. Custom RAL colors imported profiles par available hain. Black aur grey abhi sabse popular hain modern homes mein.

## FAQ — Aluminium Window Grills

**Q1. Aluminium grill aur iron/MS grill mein kya choose karein?**
Aluminium: 100% rust-proof, lightweight (30% lighter), powder coat finish 15+ saal. Price ₹350–495/sqft. Iron/MS: raw strength zyada hai, ornamental designs possible, price ₹220–330/sqft lekin har 2–3 saal painting zaroori. Ground floor security ke liye iron + aluminium combination bhi popular hai.

**Q2. Window grill ke liye konsa profile size best hai?**
12mm round: minimal, modern look, standard windows ke liye. 19×19mm square: clean geometric look, most popular abhi. 25×25mm square: heavy-duty, ground floor security ke liye. 12×38mm flat: contemporary flat bar design. Profile size se price directly affect hoti hai — 12mm se 25mm tak ₹385 se ₹495/sqft.

**Q3. Powder coating kitne time mein fade hoti hai?**
Premium Jotun ya AkzoNobel powder coat, 200°C pe bake kiya — 15+ saal chalti hai. Texture finish scratch-resistant hoti hai. Wooden grain finish fading-resistant hai. Coastal areas mein thodi jaldi fade ho sakti hai — wahan anodized finish better hai. Hum sirf top brands use karte hain.

**Q4. Grill lगाने ke baad window open ho sakti hai?**
Haan — andar ki taraf openable grills available hain with concealed bolt mounting. Ek special key se khul jaati hai window cleaning ke liye. ₹50–80/sqft extra cost aata hai. Standard fixed grills bhi laga sakte hain — woh thodi sasti hain.

**Q5. Threaded iron rod kyun add karte hain aluminium grill mein?**
8mm ya 10mm iron rods grill ki anti-cut strength 3x badhate hain. Without rod: 80–100kg impact force. With 10mm rod: 150kg+ impact force. Ground floor windows aur high-crime areas ke liye recommend karte hain. Extra cost ₹30–50/sqft.

**Q6. Balcony grill aur window grill mein kya difference hai?**
Window grill: lighter profiles (12–19mm), closer spacing, 1.2–1.5mm thickness. Balcony safety grill: thicker profiles (19–25mm), 1.5–2.0mm thickness, child-safe spacing (max 100mm gap), full-height design. Balcony grill ₹440–605/sqft vs window grill ₹350–495/sqft.

**Q7. Custom size aur design mein kitna time lagta hai?**
Standard designs: 7–10 days delivery. Custom geometric patterns: 12–15 days. Ornamental iron grills: 15–20 days. Measurement visit free hai. Production hamare Hyderabad workshop mein hoti hai.

**Q8. Grill ke saath window ka ventilation affect hota hai?**
Bilkul nahi — aluminium grill ke pipes ke beech kaafi space rehta hai airflow ke liye. Standard 2-inch gap design mein 70%+ air pass hota hai. Mosquito mesh add karni ho toh grill ke andar ki taraf separate frame mein fit hoti hai.

## FAQ — Folding & Bifold Doors

**Q1. Folding door aur sliding door mein kya fark hai?**
Sliding door: maximum 50% opening milta hai — ek panel hamesha block karta hai. Folding/bifold door: 90–95% opening milta hai — sare panels ek side stack ho jaate hain accordion ki tarah. Balcony ya garden connection ke liye folding door clearly better choice hai.

**Q2. Bi-fold aur fold-and-slide mein kya choose karein?**
Bi-fold (50mm/52mm): 2–6 panels, balcony/patio ke liye, RAL color options, ₹1,750–2,850/sqft. Fold & Slide (imported slim): 2 panels only, entrance gates/showroom fronts ke liye, Matt Black/Gold/Rose Gold finish, ₹1,550–2,150/sqft. Large residential opening = bi-fold, entrance gate = fold & slide.

**Q3. 2+1 aur 3+1 configuration ka kya matlab hai?**
Number before "+" = panels jo folded/parked rehte hain. Number after "+" = main daily-use panel (normal door ki tarah use karte hain). Example: 3+1 mein +1 panel se daily entry, 3 panels unfold karo jab full opening chahiye. Ye telescopic door se alag hai — folding mein koi fixed panel nahi hota.

**Q4. Monsoon mein paani andar aata hai folding door se?**
Nahi — weatherproof EPDM gasket sealing har panel ke beech aur frame ke around lagti hai. Bottom track mein drainage channels hote hain jo water redirect karte hain. Indian monsoon conditions mein 10+ saal test kiya hua design hai. Tight seal ensure karta hai ki drive karne wali barish bhi andar na aaye.

**Q5. Folding door ki security kaisi hai?**
Bi-fold doors mein toughened safety glass (easily nahi toot-ta) + mortice lock frame mein built-in. Multi-point locking system top, middle, bottom pe engage karta hai. Ground floor ke liye laminated safety glass recommend karte hain jo cut-proof bhi hoti hai.

**Q6. Panels ki maximum size kya hoti hai?**
Standard panel width: 600mm–900mm per panel. Maximum panel height: 3000mm (10ft). Very wide openings ke liye 4 ya 6 panels use karte hain. Calculator mein size enter karo toh exact panel configuration auto-suggest hota hai.

**Q7. Installation kitne din mein hoti hai?**
4-panel standard balcony: 1 din. 6+ panels: 1.5–2 din. Pre-fabricated components aate hain exact measurements par cut karke — site par mostly assembly aur alignment hoti hai. Same din use kar sakte hain installation complete hone ke baad.

**Q8. Kya kitchen ke liye folding door suitable hai?**
Kitchen ke liye smell partition chahiye? Telescopic door (3+1 ya 4+1) better hai — 50–70% opening, ek side fixed panel. Full balcony connection chahiye? Bifold better. Kitchen + dining room ke beech slim folding partition bhi popular hai — ₹1,200–1,800/sqft mein aata hai.

# CATEGORY 4: SHOWER PARTITIONS / GLASS

## FAQ — Shower Partitions & Glass Enclosures

**Q1. Shower partition ke liye konsa glass best hai?**
Minimum 8mm toughened (tempered) glass use karna chahiye — building codes bhi yehi kehte hain. 10mm aur 12mm thicker feel deta hai aur zyada sturdy lagta hai. Frosted ya etched glass privacy ke liye. Clear glass chhota bathroom bada dikhata hai. DGU shower mein zarurat nahi — single toughened hi fine hai.

**Q2. Frameless aur semi-frameless shower enclosure mein kya choose karein?**
Frameless: no aluminium frame, pure glass look, cleaning aasaan, ₹800–1,200/sqft. Premium feel. Semi-frameless: top aur bottom mein thin aluminium frame, cost ₹600–900/sqft, slightly more water-tight. Full-frame: traditional look, ₹400–700/sqft, most affordable. Modern bathrooms ke liye frameless recommend karte hain.

**Q3. Shower glass clean karna kitna mushkil hai?**
Toughened glass pe anti-calcium coating available hai — ye hard water deposits (limescale) 80% kam karta hai. Frameless design mein corners nahi hote jahan grime accumulate ho. Weekly ek cloth wipe sufficient hai. Anti-fungal silicone sealant lagaate hain jo 5–7 saal chalti hai.

**Q4. Shower partition install karne ke baad kitni jaldi use kar sakte hain?**
Silicone curing time 24 hours hai. Hum recommend karte hain 24 ghante wait karein first use se pehle. Hardware (hinges, handles) immediately after installation use kar sakte hain — sirf silicone joints ko time chahiye.

**Q5. Walk-in shower aur enclosed shower cabin mein kya better hai?**
Walk-in: open design, no door needed (L-shape ya straight panel), minimum 900mm width needed, ₹700–1,100/sqft. Easier to clean, modern look. Enclosed cabin: door ke saath, better water containment, smaller bathrooms ke liye suitable, ₹900–1,400/sqft. Small bathroom = enclosed, large modern bathroom = walk-in.

**Q6. PVD coating wala hardware kya hota hai?**
PVD (Physical Vapour Deposition) coating = gold, rose gold, black, chrome finish jo rust-proof aur scratch-resistant hai. Normal chrome plating 2–3 saal mein peel hoti hai — PVD 15+ saal chalti hai. Shower environment mein daily water + soap exposure hoti hai isliye PVD hardware invest worth hai. Extra cost ₹3,000–8,000 per set hardware.

# CATEGORY 5: GLASS ELEVATION / STRUCTURAL GLAZING
# ════════════════════════════════════════

## FAQ — Glass Elevation & Structural Glazing

**Q1. Structural glazing aur curtain wall mein kya fark hai?**
Structural glazing: glass directly aluminium frame se silicon bond se judta hai — frame dikhti nahi bahar se. Seamless glass look. Curtain wall: glass panels aluminium grid system mein fit hote hain — thin visible grid pattern hota hai. Commercial buildings mein curtain wall common hai, luxury villas mein structural glazing preferred hai.

**Q2. Reflector glass aur clear glass mein se kya choose karein elevation ke liye?**
Reflector (tinted): building bahar se mirror jaisi dikhti hai, privacy milti hai, heat 30–40% kam reflect hoti hai, ₹900–1,200/sqft. Clear: maximum light inside, transparent look, ₹800–1,000/sqft. DGU (double glazed): best insulation, quietest, ₹1,100–1,500/sqft. North/east facing = clear glass, south/west facing = reflector ya DGU.

**Q3. Glass elevation mein leakage nahi hoti kya?**
Structural silicone sealant + EPDM gaskets double protection dete hain. Proper drainage channels design mein included hote hain. Annual inspection aur silicone resealing (10–15 saal ke baad) se leakage issues nahi aate. Wind-driven rain mein bhi watertight rehta hai agar proper installation ho.

**Q4. Glass elevation ka maintenance kitna aata hai?**
Cleaning: professional glass cleaning ₹5–15/sqft quarterly. Silicone resealing: ₹50–100/sqft ek baar 10–15 saal mein. Hardware lubrication: annual, negligible cost. Overall: 20+ saal mein paint, waterproofing jaise traditional maintenance expenses nahi hote.

**Q5. Wind load aur earthquake resistance kya hoti hai?**
6mm toughened glass: IS 875 standard ke liye sufficient hai majority India mein. High-rise ya coastal areas: DGU ya laminated glass recommend karte hain. Spider glazing (point-fixed) mein stainless steel fittings flex kar sakte hain seismic activity mein. Structural engineer consultation zaruri hai 3rd floor se upar ke projects ke liye.

# CATEGORY 6: HPL / ACP CLADDING
# ════════════════════════════════════════

## FAQ — HPL & ACP Elevation Cladding

**Q1. HPL aur ACP mein kya difference hai?**
ACP (Aluminium Composite Panel): aluminium skin + plastic/mineral core, ₹300–500/sqft, lightweight, easy installation, limited fire rating. HPL (High Pressure Laminate): compressed resin + paper layers, ₹500–900/sqft, better weather resistance, UV fade resistant, higher fire rating. Premium facades ke liye HPL, budget commercial buildings ke liye ACP.

**Q2. ACP panel mein fire safety kya hoti hai?**
Standard ACP (PE core): non-fire rated — commercial buildings mein restricted hai. FR (Fire Retardant) ACP: slow burn, ₹50–100/sqft extra — most commercial projects ke liye acceptable. A2 mineral core ACP: near non-combustible, highest safety, ₹150–250/sqft extra — high-rise buildings ke liye mandatory in many states. Hum sirf FR ya A2 core recommend karte hain.

**Q3. HPL cladding kitne saal chalti hai?**
Interior HPL: 15–20 saal. Exterior HPL (weather-grade): 10–15 saal with UV protection. Premium brands (Trespa, Abet): 20+ saal. Annual cleaning sufficient hai. Colour fade: HPL mein minimal (5–8% over 10 years), ACP mein slightly more.

**Q4. Kya cladding purane building par bhi lag sakti hai?**
Haan — renovation mein cladding popular hai. Old brick/plaster facade par directly aluminium sub-frame attach hoti hai, phir panels fit hote hain. Building ka look completely transform hota hai. Water-resistant layer pehle lagaate hain. Existing building weight calculation zaroori hai.

# CATEGORY 7: METAL LOUVERS / PERGOLA
# ════════════════════════════════════════

## FAQ — Metal Louvers & Pergola

**Q1. Aluminium louver aur wooden louver mein kya choose karein?**
Aluminium louver: rust-proof, no maintenance, anodized ya powder coat finish, ₹800–1,500/sqft, 20+ saal. Wooden louver: natural look, lekin termites, moisture, painting maintenance. India ke outdoor conditions mein aluminium clearly better choice hai long-term ke liye.

**Q2. Fixed louver aur motorized/adjustable louver mein kya fark hai?**
Fixed louver: angle set hota hai manufacturing mein — light aur privacy control limited. ₹800–1,200/sqft. Adjustable manual: blade angle manually set karo — flexible. ₹1,000–1,500/sqft. Motorized (BLDC): app ya remote se control, rain/wind sensors automatic, ₹1,300–2,500/sqft. Terrace pergola ke liye motorized most convenient hai.

**Q3. Pergola ke liye kitne sqft ka budget rakhein?**
10×12ft pergola structure: ₹1,50,000–2,50,000. 15×15ft: ₹3,00,000–4,50,000. 20×20ft: ₹5,00,000–8,00,000. Glass roof additional ₹800–1,200/sqft. Motorized roof additional ₹50,000–1,50,000 depending on size. Free calculator on site se exact BOQ milta hai.

**Q4. Louvers rain-proof hote hain?**
Fixed louvers mein gaps hote hain — partial rain protection. Adjustable louvers close position pe near-watertight hote hain. Proper drainage gutter system integrate karte hain toh rain completely manage ho jaata hai. Motorized systems mein rain sensor automatic close kar deta hai louvers ko.

**Q5. Terrace pergola mein konsa roof best hai?**
Polycarbonate: lightest, cheapest (₹300–600/sqft), some heat reduction. Toughened glass: clearest view, best aesthetics, ₹600–1,000/sqft. DGU glass: best heat insulation, ₹900–1,400/sqft. Fabric retractable: folds away completely, premium look, ₹1,200–2,000/sqft. For Hyderabad/South India summer: DGU glass best hai.

# CATEGORY 8: GLASS RAILINGS
# ════════════════════════════════════════

## FAQ — Glass Railings

**Q1. Glass railing safe hai kya — agar glass toot jaaye?**
Haan — hum sirf toughened (tempered) glass use karte hain jo normal glass se 5x stronger hai. Toot-ne par chhote blunt pieces banta hai (sharp shards nahi) — injury nahi hoti. Laminated glass option bhi hai — toot-ne par film hold karta hai pieces ko. SS304 brackets aur nut-bolt assembly use karte hain — no welding, no silicon dependence.

**Q2. Glass railing mein konsa glass use karna chahiye?**
Minimum: 10mm toughened glass. Recommended: 12mm toughened (standard height balcony). Premium: 12mm + 12mm laminated (sandwich). Height 1 meter tak ke liye 10mm sufficient, 1 meter se upar ke liye 12mm mandatory as per NBC guidelines.

**Q3. SS304 vs SS316 bracket mein kya choose karein?**
SS304: standard stainless steel, 99% India buildings ke liye sufficient, ₹800–1,200/bracket. SS316: marine grade, extra corrosion resistance — coastal areas (Mumbai, Chennai, Goa) ke liye recommend karte hain. ₹1,200–1,800/bracket. Inland cities mein SS304 perfectly fine hai.

**Q4. Glass railing ke saath top handrail zaroori hai kya?**
Building codes mein 1 meter+ height balcony ke liye handrail required hai. Glass ke upar aluminium channel ya SS pipe handrail lagti hai. Frameless look ke liye top glass channel popular hai — glass hi handrail ka kaam karta hai. NBC 2016 guidelines follow karte hain hum.

**Q5. Glass railing ki cleaning kaise karein?**
Weekly: glass cleaner + microfiber cloth. SS hardware: steel-specific cleaner quarterly. Anti-calcium coating optionally add kar sakte hain glass par — hard water stains 80% reduce hoti hain. Frameless design mein cleaning aasaan hai kyunki corners kam hain.
