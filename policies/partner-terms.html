Frequently asked questions

Is WoodenMax responsible if a partner gives poor service or a defective product?

No. WoodenMax is a listing platform only. Orders, payments, delivery, installation, warranty and after-sales service are solely between the partner and the customer. We do, however, review complaints and may suspend or remove partners with repeated unresolved issues.

Can I be listed without GST?

Yes. If you don't have GST, you can apply using an Udyam / MSME (Laghu Udyam) registration (or an equivalent business registration if you are outside India).

Does the free listing make me a WoodenMax employee, agent or franchise?

No. A listing creates no employment, agency, partnership, franchise or joint-venture relationship with WoodenMax.

How do I get my listing removed?

Email info@woodenmax.com from your registered email and we'll remove it promptly.
