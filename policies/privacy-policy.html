2. Cookies & tracking

We use three categories of cookies:

  • Essential — calculator project estimate (saved configurations), language, cookie consent itself. Cannot be turned off; the site won't work without them.
  • Analytics — Google Analytics 4 (anonymised IP), Microsoft Clarity (privacy mode). You can turn these off via our cookie banner.
  • Marketing — Meta Pixel, Google Ads conversion. You can turn these off via our cookie banner.

We do not use cross-site advertising tracking. We do not buy or sell data through Data Management Platforms.

3. How long we keep your data

  • Quote requests not converted to orders: 18 months, then anonymised
  • Customer orders: 8 years (statutory GST audit requirement)
  • Call recordings: 90 days
  • WhatsApp chats: as per Meta retention defaults (up to 7 years)
  • Web analytics: 14 months at Google's end
  • Marketing cookies: 365 days max, often less

5. Your rights under DPDP 2023

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you the following rights — and we honour all of them, regardless of whether the rules are notified for our category yet:

  • Right to access — get a copy of every record we hold about you
  • Right to correct — fix any inaccurate detail
  • Right to erase — request deletion (within 30 days; some data retained per statutory rules above)
  • Right to nominate — choose someone to act on your behalf in case of incapacity
  • Right to grievance redressal — escalate to our Data Protection Officer (below)

Material changes are communicated by email to every customer with an active account 30 days before they take effect. Minor edits (typos, contact details) take effect immediately and are noted in the change log at the bottom of this page.

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell my data to other window companies for lead resale?

Absolutely not. We have never resold a single lead. Doing so would destroy our reputation and is contractually prohibited by the agreements we sign with our customers.

I gave my number on the price calculator. Why am I getting WhatsApp messages from your sales team?

Because by submitting the form you opted in to receive a quote PDF and follow-up. You can reply STOP to the WhatsApp thread or write to dpo@woodenmax.in to opt out — we close the loop within 24 hours.

Can I get a copy of every record you hold about me?

Yes. Email dpo@woodenmax.in from the address you originally signed up with. We deliver a ZIP file with your form submissions, call notes, WhatsApp chats and orders within 7 working days.

Where is my data stored?

Primary database — AWS Mumbai region (ap-south-1). Backups — AWS Hyderabad region (ap-south-2). Both are within India. Email is on Google Workspace which keeps copies on EU servers under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision.

I want to be deleted from your CRM permanently.

Send a request to dpo@woodenmax.in. Within 30 days, we remove all records except those we are legally bound to keep (e.g., invoices for GST audit — 8 years per Section 36 of the CGST Act). Those are anonymised wherever possible and access-restricted.
