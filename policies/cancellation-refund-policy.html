Cancellation & Refund Policy

Custom-fabricated aluminium has different cancellation rules than e-commerce. This page tells you exactly what you can cancel, when, and how much comes back.

  • Free to cancel before signing the final quote — no questions
  • 10% retention if cancelled after token, before production starts
  • Cancellation locked once we cut the first profile (fully customised goods)
  • Refunds processed within 7 working days to source account

Why production-stage cancellation is locked
Every WoodenMax order is fabricated to your exact opening size, your selected colour and your hardware combination. Once profiles are cut and powder-coated, they cannot be re-sold to another customer because the size + colour combination is yours alone. This is industry-standard for custom fabrication.

2. Refund timelines

  • Day 0: You raise a cancellation in writing (email is fine)
  • Day 1: Our accounts team acknowledges and confirms the refundable amount
  • Day 2–5: Refund is initiated via the original payment method (NEFT / RTGS / UPI / card reversal)
  • Day 5–7: Refund reflects in your account, depending on your bank
  • Refunds to international cards may take up to 14 working days due to inter-bank routing

3. Partial cancellations — removing items from a multi-item order

If you placed an order for, say, 12 windows and want to drop 2 from the scope before production:

  • If the dropped items have not been measured / shop-drawn yet → no charge, scope updated
  • If measured but not yet in production → ₹ 1,200 per opening design/measurement fee
  • If in production → full charge applies for those items; you can choose to receive them and stock them, or we recycle and you forfeit

4. Cancellation by WoodenMax — when we cancel an order

Very rare, but for transparency:

  • Site is found to be structurally unsafe for the requested system (e.g., requesting a 4×3 m sliding window in an opening that lacks the required load-bearing lintel)
  • Customer's site is not accessible (legal dispute, locked site, persistent denial of entry by association)
  • Payment chargebacks raised maliciously after successful installation

In all such cases we refund any advance immediately, minus only the documented design/drawing time.

5. Refund method

Refunds are processed strictly to the same account / instrument used for original payment. We do not transfer refunds to a different account because of GST audit and anti-money-laundering compliance under section 17 of the IGST Act.

If a cancellation invoice involves a GST reversal, our team files the corresponding credit note in your GSTR and shares a copy with you. This is essential for B2B (GST-registered) customers — your input-tax-credit is preserved.

Frequently asked questions

I changed my mind a week after paying the token but before manufacturing — what do I get back?

You receive 90% of your token back. The 10% retention covers the design time, shop-drawing creation, measurement on site, and our accounting effort. Refund hits your account in 7 working days.

My builder cancelled the project, can I cancel?

Yes, the cancellation policy applies regardless of the reason. If your builder agreement compensates you for vendor commitments, we are happy to provide a notarised acceptance letter to support your claim.

You took my 50% advance and never installed. How do I get a refund?

That would be an exceptional service failure — not in our memory, but the path is: email info@woodenmax.com. Founder-level review and refund within 3 working days. Beyond that, every order is also covered under our dispute redressal mechanism.

Can you give the refund in cash?

No. All refunds are by bank transfer back to the original payment source. This is required by RBI and GST regulations.

I want to change colour of my windows after token but before production. Charge?

If the change is requested within 48 hours of token payment, free. After 48 hours but before profile is cut, ₹ 1,200 per opening as a re-spec fee. After cutting, the cost of new profile.
