In India's hot climate, AC bills account for a significant portion of household electricity bills. Energy efficient windows (DGU, Low-E glass, thermal break) can reduce AC bills by 20-30% — especially beneficial in cities like Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore.

Understanding Energy Loss Through Windows

Traditional single-pane windows are major sources of heat gain during summer and heat loss during winter. In India, the primary concern is heat gain, which forces your AC to work harder. Energy-efficient windows address this problem through advanced glass technology and frame design.

Types of Energy-Efficient Windows

Double Glazed Units (DGU)

Two panes with air gap reduce heat transfer by 40-50%. See DGU windows and slim profile windows with DGU options.

Low-E Glass Coating

Reflects heat while allowing visible light through

Thermal Break Frames

Insulating barrier prevents heat conduction

1. Double Glazed Units (DGU)

DGU windows feature two panes of glass with an air or gas-filled gap between them. This design significantly reduces heat transfer:

  • 🔹 Reduces heat gain by 40-50% compared to single-pane windows
  • 🔹 Improves thermal insulation
  • 🔹 Reduces noise by 30-40 dB
  • 🔹 Prevents condensation

2. Low-E Glass Coating

Low-emissivity (Low-E) glass has a special coating that reflects heat while allowing visible light to pass through. This is ideal for Indian homes facing direct sunlight.

3. Thermal Break Aluminium Frames

Thermal break technology uses insulating materials to separate interior and exterior frame parts, preventing heat conduction through metal frames.

Expert Recommendation: For balconies and windows facing east or west in Indian cities, DGU with Low-E coating provides maximum energy savings. The initial investment pays for itself within 3-5 years through reduced electricity bills.

What Factors Affect the Price?

The price of energy efficient windows depends on several factors. If you need a clear estimate, it's important to understand these factors:

  • Glass Type: Single glass (₹400-600/sqft), DGU (₹800-1,200/sqft), DGU with Low-E (₹1,000-1,400/sqft), Triple-pane (₹1,400-1,800/sqft)
  • Low-E Coating: Standard DGU (₹800-1,200/sqft), DGU with Low-E (+₹200-300/sqft)
  • Thermal Break: Standard frame (base), Thermal break frame (+₹100-200/sqft)
  • Profile Series: 27mm Domal (₹550-950/sqft), 29mm Premium (₹1,200-1,400/sqft), Slim Profile (₹900-1,400/sqft)
  • Air Gap Size: 6mm (standard), 12mm (enhanced), 16mm (maximum) — larger gap = better insulation
  • Installation Type: Professional installation (5-10% of total cost) with proper sealing
  • Location & Transport: Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore — transport costs vary by location

Common FAQs

1. How is energy efficient window price calculated?

To calculate price: (Width × Height in sq.ft) × Price per sq.ft. Example: 200 sq.ft window area. If price is ₹1,100/sq.ft (DGU with Low-E), then total = 200 × ₹1,100 = ₹2,20,000. Glass upgrades, thermal break, profile series, and installation costs are added to this.

2. DGU vs Low-E glass — which is better?

DGU (Double Glazed Unit) reduces heat transfer by 40-50% and provides thermal insulation — most cost-effective solution. Low-E coating with DGU reduces heat transfer by 30-40% (total 60-70% reduction) — ideal for maximum energy savings. Low-E coating costs +₹200-300/sqft extra, but it's worth it in the long term.

3. How much AC bill savings will there be?

DGU windows provide 20-30% AC bill reduction. Example: If monthly AC bill is ₹10,000, after DGU it will be ₹7,000-8,000 — ₹2,000-3,000 monthly savings. Annual savings ₹24,000-36,000. For 200 sq.ft window area, ₹15,000-30,000 annual savings is possible, depending on AC usage and location.

4. What is thermal break and why is it necessary?

Thermal break is an insulating material that separates interior and exterior frame parts — prevents heat conduction through metal frames. Without thermal break, aluminium frames act as heat conductors. Thermal break costs +₹100-200/sqft extra, but provides 10-15% additional energy savings.

5. What is the payback period?

Energy efficient windows have a payback period of 3-5 years. Example: ₹1,20,000 additional investment, ₹22,500 annual savings = 5.3 years payback. After this, it's pure savings. Significant savings over 20-25 years lifespan — long-term investment is worth it.

6. Is there a price difference in Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Delhi?

Base prices are similar, but transport costs vary by location. Transport costs may be higher in Mumbai and Delhi NCR. You can save on transport costs by purchasing directly from local vendors. Installation costs also vary by location.
