WoodenMax offers 8 major product categories with 25+ specific products for homes, villas, and commercial buildings. From budget-friendly 3-track sliding windows to premium structural glazing, this guide helps you understand each product, its best use case, and approximate pricing.

1. Aluminium Windows

Our largest category with 10+ products. Aluminium windows range from economy 27mm to premium slim profiles. Key products:

  • 3-Track Sliding Window (27mm Domal) — ₹550-950/sqft, max ventilation, mesh option
  • 29mm Premium Aluminium Sliding Window — ₹1,200-1,400/sqft, premium homes
  • 2-Track French Sliding Door — ₹1,850-2,250/sqft, 50% opening, luxury balconies
  • Slimline Aluminium Window — ₹900-1,400/sqft, premium look
  • Top-Hung Casement, Georgian Grill Casement, Slim Entrance Door, Full Elevation Villa Facade

Best for: Balconies, bedrooms, living rooms, villas. Choose profile based on budget and aesthetics.

2. Telescope Windows

Telescope windows offer panels that slide and stack — maximum opening with slim frames. Single product:

  • Telescopic Slim Sliding Door — ₹950+/sqft, 80%+ opening, luxury balconies & patios

Best for: Large openings where you want more opening than standard sliding (50%) but less cost than folding (95%).

3. Folding Systems

Folding systems provide up to 95% opening — panels fold and stack to one side. Ideal for seamless indoor-outdoor living.

  • Bi-Fold Aluminium Doors — ₹800-1,400/sqft, luxury balconies
  • Fold & Slide Window System — hybrid option for large openings

Best for: Villas, large balconies, patios. Compare with sliding vs folding before choosing.

4. Metal Louvers

Metal louvers — adjustable aluminium blades for shade, ventilation, and facade design. Price per piece (₹700-1,500).

  • Wooden Finish Aluminium Louvers — wood look, exterior facades
  • Curved Architectural Louvers — curved designs
  • Ceiling Pergola Louvers — outdoor pergolas
  • Louver Canopy Facade — canopy & facade combination

Best for: Building facades, sun control, modern architecture.

5. Shower Partitions

Shower partitions — frameless to framed, ₹400-1,200/sqft. Toughened glass, various profiles.

  • Frameless Shower Partition — premium, minimal frame
  • Black Profile Shower, Premium Black Profile
  • Slim Frame Shower, Frosted Glass Bathroom Door

Best for: Bathrooms, wet areas. Frameless = premium; framed = budget-friendly.

6. Elevation Cladding

Elevation cladding — HPL and ACP for building exteriors. ₹120+/sqft.

  • HPL Exterior Cladding — durable, various finishes
  • HPL + ACP Elevation Cladding — combined systems

Best for: Building facades, villas, commercial exteriors.

7. Glass Railing

Glass railing — ₹600-1,200/sqft. Toughened glass with minimal frames.

  • Balcony Glass Railing — balconies, terraces
  • Staircase Glass Railing — stair safety with clear view

Best for: Balconies, staircases, terraces. Combines safety with unobstructed views.

8. Glass Elevation

Glass elevation — curtain wall, structural glazing, spider glazing. ₹800-1,200/sqft. Premium building facades.

  • Aluminium Frame: ₹800-1,000/sqft — standard commercial
  • Structural Glazing: ₹900-1,200/sqft — frameless look
  • Spider Glazing: ₹1,200+/sqft — point-fixed, premium

Best for: Commercial buildings, luxury villas, modern facades.

How to Choose the Right Product

Quick selection guide:
  • Balcony/Patio: Sliding (budget) or Folding (max opening)
  • Bedroom/Living: 3-Track or 29mm Sliding
  • Bathroom: Frameless or Slim Frame shower
  • Building Facade: Glass Elevation or HPL Cladding
  • Balcony Safety: Glass Railing
