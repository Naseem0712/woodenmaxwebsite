# Fix J — Competitor SERP title / H1 benchmarks

**Research date:** 2026-11-06 (WebSearch + page fetches)  
**Scope:** Product vs price vs hub/system pages — Alupure (priority), Fenesta, Encraft, Alteza, Alcoi, Schüco, Saint-Gobain  
**Use:** Shape WoodenMax CTR titles/H1s. **Do not** paste competitor brand / series trademarks into WoodenMax titles or copy.

## 8. Cross-brand pattern → WoodenMax title formulas

**Do not** use competitor brand names, series names (AluBUILD, AluLITE, ACA ##, AS 39, Invisia, AluPure, etc.), or trademark spellings in WoodenMax titles.

### Usable WoodenMax formulas (cite these in A–D / K–N rewrites)

```
PRODUCT (PDP):
  {Distinct modifier} {Product type} | Soft Designs & Price Band | WoodenMax
  Example: 3-Track Aluminium Sliding Window with Mesh | Soft Price Band | WoodenMax

PRICE GUIDE:
  {Product type} Price in India ({year}) | Soft ₹/sqft Band | WoodenMax
  Example: Frameless Glass Shower Price (2026) | Soft ₹/sqft Band | WoodenMax

HUB (browse):
  {Category} for Indian Homes | Types, Soft Price Bands & Calculators | WoodenMax
  Example: Shower Partitions for Indian Bathrooms | Types, Soft Bands & Calculators | WoodenMax

SYSTEM / SERIES (when we own a named WoodenMax line — not competitor codes):
  {Our series or track identity} {Product type} | Spec Guide | WoodenMax

CITY PRICE (Fix H style):
  {Product type} Price in {City} | {Local proof cue} & Soft Band | WoodenMax
```

### Role split (must keep)

| Intent | Owns “Price” / ₹ cue | Owns type / modifier | Owns browse |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------|-------------|
| Product PDP | Soft “Price” / band only | Modifier + type | Links up to hub |
| Price guide | Strong “Price / ₹/sqft” | Type only | Links to PDP |
| Hub | Soft “bands & calculators” | Category plural | Links down to children |

### CTR rules distilled from competitors

1. **One primary noun** in title (sliding window / casement / shower partition) — don’t pipe three product types.
2. Put the **money modifier first** when that is the query (3-Track, Frameless, LED, Retractable).
3. Soft commercial cue (`Designs & Price`, `Soft Price Band`) beats exact ₹ in product titles.
4. Exact ₹/sqft belongs on **price guides** and FAQ answers from the canonical rate source.
5. Hubs never steal a child SKU title; children never flatten into generic hub wording.
