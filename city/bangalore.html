Questions from Bangalore Customers

What is the price of aluminium windows in Bangalore?
Aluminium window prices in Bangalore start from ₹550/sqft for basic 2-track sliding windows. Premium slim profile windows range from ₹900-1400/sqft depending on profile and glass selection.
Do you install windows in Bangalore?
WoodenMax provides PAN India online price calculator and technical consultation. Physical installation is currently available in Bangalore. For Bangalore, we share specifications with trusted local fabricators.
Which aluminium sliding window is best for Bangalore weather?
Bangalore's moderate climate suits most window types. For better ventilation, we recommend casement or top-hung windows. For dust protection, 3-track sliding with mesh is ideal.
What glass options are available?
We offer clear toughened glass (5-12mm), tinted glass for sun control, DGU for noise reduction, laminated safety glass, and decorative fluted glass options.
How much does a villa facade cost in Bangalore?
Full glass elevation for Bangalore villas ranges from ₹1320-2200/sqft including slim aluminium frames, toughened glass, and premium hardware. Use our calculator for exact estimates.
What is the delivery time for windows in Bangalore?
Standard windows take 10-15 days for fabrication. Custom designs with imported profiles may require 20-25 days. Delivery timeline depends on local fabricator availability.
Can I use calculators if I am not in India?
Yes. Bangalore and India keep ₹ as the reference; tools work with no login in feet, metres, inches, cm, or mm. From abroad you may see an FX-based planning guide; supply stays India-first—contact us for export.
