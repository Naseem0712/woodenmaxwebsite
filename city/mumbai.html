For Mumbai's humid coastal climate, we recommend powder-coated aluminium profiles with marine-grade finish. DGU glass helps with insulation and condensation control.

For Mumbai high-rises above 10 floors, we recommend minimum 10mm toughened glass or laminated safety glass. DGU units provide additional wind resistance and noise reduction.
