Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of aluminium grills per sqft in India 2026?

Aluminium grill prices range from ₹300 to ₹600 per sqft depending on profile size, thickness, coating finish, and design complexity. Window grills start at ₹750/sqft while balcony safety grills start at ₹865/sqft. Use our free calculator for exact pricing.

Which is better: aluminium grills or iron grills?

Aluminium grills are rust-proof, lightweight, and require zero maintenance. Iron grills are cheaper initially (₹220-330/sqft) but need regular painting. For long-term value, aluminium grills are the better choice.

How to calculate grill price for my window size?

Use our free online grill calculator on any product page. Enter your window width and height, select profile type, thickness, and coating finish. The calculator gives you an instant price estimate including material, labour, and installation costs.

What profiles are available for aluminium grills?

We offer rectangular profiles (12x12mm to 25x50mm), round profiles (12mm, 15mm, 18mm), and oval profiles (15x38mm). Outer frame profiles range from 12x12mm to 25x50mm. Thickness options: 1.2mm, 1.5mm, 1.6mm, 2.0mm, and 2.2mm.

What coating finishes are available?

Three finishes: Plain (standard powder coat), Texture (adds ₹15/sqft for a matte textured look), and Wooden Grain (adds ₹70/sqft for a wood-like appearance). All finishes use premium Jotun or AkzoNobel powders baked at 200°C.

How much does it cost to install grills for a full house?

For a standard 3BHK flat with 6-8 windows and 2-3 balconies, total grill cost ranges from ₹25,000 to ₹60,000 depending on the grill type. Aluminium grills with basic profiles cost around ₹35,000. Use our calculator on each product page for exact sizing.

Do you provide grill installation service across India?

We provide manufacturing and installation services in Hyderabad, Bangalore, Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Jaipur, and Lucknow. For other cities, we ship the grills with a detailed installation guide. Local fabricators can install using our precision-cut profiles.
