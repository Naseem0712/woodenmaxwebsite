Materials & safety — which grill type suits your home?

Vertical and horizontal grill designs are available in aluminium, iron and brass. Aluminium is rust-free and lightweight — ideal for upper floors and coastal homes. Iron is the strongest and most economical choice for ground-floor windows. Brass gives a premium look for main entrances and designer facades.

Round bar grills are made only in solid iron bars. Solid round bars deliver up to 100% safety — they cannot be bent or cut easily and have the longest service life. Best for high-security windows and shop fronts.

Solid bar grills allow any custom design in solid bars for maximum security — ground floor homes, ATMs, store fronts and gates. We fabricate to your drawing or site measurement.

Which is better: aluminium grills or iron grills?

Aluminium grills are rust-proof, lightweight, and require zero maintenance. Iron grills are cheaper initially (₹220-330/sqft) but need regular painting. For long-term value, aluminium grills are the better choice.

What profiles are available for aluminium grills?

We offer rectangular profiles (12x12mm to 25x50mm), round profiles (12mm, 15mm, 18mm), and oval profiles (15x38mm). Outer frame profiles range from 12x12mm to 25x50mm. Thickness options: 1.2mm, 1.5mm, 1.6mm, 2.0mm, and 2.2mm.

What coating finishes are available?

Three finishes: Plain (standard powder coat), Texture (adds ₹15/sqft for a matte textured look), and Wooden Grain (adds ₹70/sqft for a wood-like appearance). All finishes use premium Jotun or AkzoNobel powders baked at 200°C.
