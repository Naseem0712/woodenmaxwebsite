C-type vs D-type

C-type mirrors cover more than a semicircle (wider arc). D-type is exactly half-round. Both use the same LED rate card and imported machining (etching, banding, cutting).

Frequently asked questions

What is a C-type mirror?

When the mirror arc is wider than a semicircle — popular for bathroom vanities and feature walls.

Why is the rate the same as D-type?

Both use the same Imported frame, connector banding and LED strip specification — billable area is width × height.

What is the hardware warranty?

Touch sensor, LED driver and LED strip carry 1 year warranty. Profile manufacturing defects are covered up to 10 years (line dependent).

Are packing and transport charges included?

Packing and transit are quoted separately after order confirmation — safe crate or foam packing based on mirror size.
