Frequently asked questions

Why is there a 1.5× wastage factor?

Custom dual-glass cutting has higher material loss — the calculator uses 1.5× billable area by default for a realistic quote.

Can I have both touch and motion sensors?

Touch sensor, LED driver and LED strip carry 1 year warranty. Profile manufacturing defects are covered up to 10 years (line dependent).

Are packing and transport charges included?

Packing and transit are quoted separately after order confirmation — safe crate or foam packing based on mirror size.

How long until dispatch?
