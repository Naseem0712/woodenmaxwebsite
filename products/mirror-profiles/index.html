Frequently asked questions

What is the starting LED mirror price per piece?

LED bathroom mirror starts from ₹720/ft for single-strip systems and goes up to ₹1,850/ft for premium warm-white double-strip profiles with dimming.

Do you supply frame only or complete LED mirror?

Both — we can supply profile-only for your glazier, or a complete mirror panel with glass, LED and installation.

Which cities do you deliver to?

Primary fabrication from Hyderabad with installation teams in Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Mumbai, Bengaluru and project-based dispatch pan-India.

Is the LED bathroom mirror waterproof?

Bathroom-grade profiles use IP-rated LED strips and sealed aluminium channels suitable for humid environments when installed correctly.

What warranty do you offer?

LED driver warranty 2 years, aluminium profile 10 years against manufacturing defects. Glass breakage in transit is covered at dispatch.

What is the hardware warranty?

Touch sensor, LED driver and LED strip carry 1 year warranty. Profile manufacturing defects are covered up to 10 years (line dependent).

Are packing and transport charges included?

Packing and transit are quoted separately after order confirmation — safe crate or foam packing based on mirror size.

How long until dispatch?

Within 7 working days after order confirmation and advance payment (once drawings are approved).

Is GST included in the calculator?

Calculator amounts are ex-GST. GST 18% is added on the invoice.
