Bi-Fold & Folding Sliding: What 2+1 or 3+1 Means Here

The same numbers are used as telescopic doors, but the job of each panel is different. On folding systems, no panel is fixed to the wall — every leaf folds and slides.

How folding configuration works

Before “+” = panels that stay folded / parked on the track until you need full width. After “+” = the main everyday panel — the one you use like a normal door (hinged swing or lead sliding leaf) for daily entry, similar to a casement or single swing door.

Example 3+1: three foldable panels + one regular-use panel. For daily coming and going you mainly use the +1 panel. When guests arrive or you want the balcony fully open, you release the folded stack so ~95% of the opening is free.

Bi-fold (multi-panel)

2, 3, 4 or 6 hinged panels on a top track. They fold like an accordion and slide to one side. Best for balconies, patios, wide living openings where you want maximum clear width.

Fold & slide (often 2+1 style)

Typically two panels on imported slim profiles — one folds behind the other for wide opening, or you use the lead panel alone for quick access. Popular for entrance gates and showrooms.

Not the same as telescopic

Telescopic 2+1 / 3+1 uses a fixed panel + sliding stack (~50–80% opening). Folding 2+1 / 3+1 uses a daily-use +1 panel + foldable panels for full opening. Compare on our telescopic guide.

What should you choose?

  • Kitchen smell partition, ~75% walk-through, fixed end panel OK → Telescopic 3+1 / 4+1
  • Balcony or garden — almost full opening → Bi-Fold calculator (4 or 6 panels)
  • Main gate — daily single-door feel + wide open sometimes → Fold & Slide calculator
  • Only ~50% opening, centre-opening sliding → Telescopic 2+2 (not bi-fold)

Example — 10–12 ft balcony (Bi-Fold)

Opening ~11 ft wide × 8 ft high → usually 4 panels. Park the stack to one side for ~95% clear. Price on the Bi-Fold calc (from around ₹1,750/sqft).

Example — 6–8 ft entrance (Fold & Slide)

Opening ~7 ft × 7–8 ft → typically 2 panels; use the lead leaf daily, fold both for wide access. Quote on the Fold & Slide calc (from around ₹2,250/sqft).

Install / process (short)

Site measure → fabricate to exact size → deliver pre-cut panels → on-site assembly & alignment. Standard 4-panel balcony: ~1 day. 6+ panels or entrance gate: ~1–2 days. Same-day use after install.

Why folding beats standard sliding (~95% vs ~50% opening)

A normal sliding door leaves one panel in the way (~50% clear). Folding panels stack to one side so the balcony or patio opens nearly fully. Choose Bi-Fold for wide residential openings; Fold & Slide for gates and showroom fronts.

What is the difference between a folding door and a sliding door?
A sliding door gives a maximum of about 50% opening — one panel always blocks part of the opening. A folding/bifold door gives 90–95% opening — all panels stack to one side like an accordion. For balcony or garden connections, a folding door is clearly the better choice.
Should I choose bi-fold or fold-and-slide?
Bi-fold (50mm/52mm): 2–6 panels, ideal for balconies and patios, RAL colour options, soft installed from around ₹1,750/sqft. Fold & Slide (imported slim): typically 2 panels, for entrance gates and showroom fronts, soft installed from around ₹2,250/sqft. Large residential openings = bi-fold; entrance gates = fold & slide.
What do 2+1 and 3+1 configurations mean?
The number before "+" is the count of panels that fold and park to the side. The number after "+" is the main daily-use panel (used like a normal door). Example: in 3+1, the +1 panel handles daily entry; unfold the three panels when you need the full opening. This differs from telescopic doors — folding systems have no fixed panel.
Do folding doors let water in during monsoon?
No — weatherproof EPDM gasket sealing runs between every panel and around the frame. Bottom tracks include drainage channels to redirect water. The design has been tested for 10+ years in Indian monsoon conditions. A tight seal keeps even wind-driven rain out.
How secure are folding doors?
Bi-fold doors use toughened safety glass (which does not shatter easily) plus a mortice lock built into the frame. Multi-point locking engages at the top, middle, and bottom. For ground-floor installations, we recommend laminated safety glass for added cut resistance.
What is the maximum panel size?
Standard panel width: 600mm–900mm per panel. Maximum panel height: 3000mm (10ft). For very wide openings, use 4 or 6 panels. Enter your size in the calculator and it will suggest the exact panel configuration.
How many days does installation take?
Standard 4-panel balcony: 1 day. 6+ panels: 1.5–2 days. Pre-fabricated components arrive cut to your exact measurements — on-site work is mostly assembly and alignment. You can use the doors the same day installation completes.
Is a folding door suitable for a kitchen?
Need a smell partition for the kitchen? A telescopic door (3+1 or 4+1) is better — 50–70% opening with one fixed side panel. Need a full balcony connection? Bi-fold is better. Slim folding partitions between kitchen and dining are also popular — confirm sizing on the live calculator.
How do folding doors actually work?
Each panel is connected to the next by hinges, and all panels run on a top track. When you push the first panel, it folds against the next, and together they slide along the track. At the end, all panels stack neatly to one side like a closed accordion, leaving your entire opening free.
What's the difference between 50mm and 52mm profiles?
Both are made from the same high-grade 6063-T6 aluminium. The 52mm profile has slightly thicker walls, giving it marginally more strength for very large panels or high-wind areas. For most residential applications, the 50mm profile is perfectly adequate and slightly more economical.
