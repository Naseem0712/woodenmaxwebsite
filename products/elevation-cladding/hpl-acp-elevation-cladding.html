ACP Elevation Cladding — Panel Specs & Fixing System Details

We offer two distinct elevation cladding solutions: ACP (Aluminium Composite Panel) for commercial projects and HPL (High Pressure Laminate) for luxury residential and villa applications. Both provide exceptional durability and aesthetic appeal, but serve different market segments and installation requirements.

ACP — Commercial Grade Cladding

ACP (Aluminium Composite Panel) is the go-to cladding material for commercial building elevations. Made with aluminium coil on both sides sandwiching a polyethylene core, ACP offers exceptional durability, weather resistance, and a sleek modern finish that transforms any commercial facade. For wooden finishes, we use PVDF (Polyvinylidene Fluoride) coating – a premium coating technology that provides superior color retention, UV resistance, and a realistic wood texture.

🏢 Ideal for Commercial Projects – Office buildings, showrooms, malls, hotels, hospitals, educational institutions, and any commercial facade requiring a premium, durable finish. Installation: 4×4 grid aluminium framework with wooden screws and silicon sealant (no rivets).

HPL — Luxury Residential & Villa Cladding

HPL (High Pressure Laminate) is a premium cladding solution designed for luxury residential projects, villas, and high-end commercial spaces. HPL panels feature a high-pressure decorative laminate surface with exceptional wood grain textures and natural finishes that mimic real wood, stone, or other premium materials. Unlike ACP, HPL offers a more premium, sophisticated aesthetic perfect for luxury homes and upscale projects.

🏡 Ideal for Luxury Projects – Villas, premium residential buildings, luxury hotels, high-end showrooms, and projects requiring sophisticated, natural-looking finishes. Installation: Growing system with rivets for secure, professional mounting.

HPL Installation — Grooving System with Rivets

HPL panels are installed using a grooving system with rivets – a specialized installation method that ensures secure, long-lasting mounting. The grooving system involves creating grooves or channels in the HPL panels, which are then secured using aluminium rivets to the supporting framework. This method provides:

  • Superior Strength: Rivets provide mechanical fastening that withstands wind loads and weather conditions
  • Clean Aesthetic: Growing system allows for minimal visible fasteners, creating a seamless appearance
  • Durability: Professional rivet installation ensures panels remain secure for decades
  • Premium Finish: Ideal for luxury projects where attention to detail matters

ACP Installation Framework

Our ACP installation uses 4×4 grid pattern aluminium profile framing. Panels are secured using aluminium profile frames, wooden screws, and silicon sealant (no rivets) for a clean, concealed fixing system that ensures watertight joints and easy panel replacement if needed. This method is ideal for commercial projects where cost-effectiveness and quick installation are priorities.

🔥 FR Grade B – Fire Retardant ACP

For Railways, Airports, Metro Stations, Malls, and Government projects, fire safety compliance is mandatory. FR Grade B ACP features a mineral-filled fire retardant core that doesn't propagate fire and is self-extinguishing.

Special Installation: Unlike regular ACP that mounts directly on walls, FR Grade B requires bracket mounting for walls and ceilings:

  • GI Brackets: 75×75×6mm with Anchor Bolts 10×100mm
  • OR Aluminium Angles: 50×50×50mm with same screws & anchor bolts

Available in 4mm thickness, Plain color only. Certified for large public projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

What coating is used on ACP panels?
Both wooden and plain color ACP panels use PVDF (Polyvinylidene Fluoride) coating which provides superior UV resistance and longer color retention. The difference is in the finish – wooden colors have realistic wood grain texture while plain colors are solid. Both have aluminium coil on both sides with color finish on one side and plain on the reverse.
What is FR Grade B ACP and when is it required?
FR Grade B ACP has a fire-retardant mineral core instead of standard PE core. It's mandatory for Railways, Airports, Metro Stations, Government buildings, and large public projects. The installation requires special brackets – GI brackets (75×75×6mm) with anchor bolts or Aluminium angles (50×50×50mm) – as panels cannot be directly mounted on walls/ceilings in these projects.
Which thickness should I choose?
3mm: Suitable for smaller panels and indoor applications. 4mm: Most commonly used for commercial facades – good balance of rigidity and cost. 6mm: Recommended for larger panels, high-wind areas, and where extra structural rigidity is needed. We'll recommend the best option based on your project requirements.
What is 4×4 grid pattern installation?
We install aluminium profile framework in a 4-feet × 4-feet grid pattern on the wall. This creates a strong, uniform backing that ensures the ACP panels remain flat and secure. Unlike riveted systems, we use wooden screws and silicon sealant for a cleaner finish and watertight joints.
What sheet size is used for calculation?
We use 4×12 feet (48 sq.ft) standard sheets for calculation. Available sizes include 4×8 ft (32 sq.ft), 4×10 ft (40 sq.ft), and 4×12 ft (48 sq.ft). Your area + 5% wastage is divided by 48 sq.ft, and the result is rounded UP to determine sheets required.
What brands do you use?
We source from top Indian ACP brands known for quality and durability. The specific brand may vary based on availability and your color/finish requirements. All sheets meet the quality standards for commercial exterior applications with proper fire-retardant cores.
What is the difference between ACP and HPL cladding?
ACP (Aluminium Composite Panel) is ideal for commercial projects like offices, malls, hotels, and showrooms. It uses aluminium coil on both sides with a PE core, installed with 4×4 grid framework using screws and sealant. HPL (High Pressure Laminate) is designed for luxury residential projects and villas, offering premium natural wood textures and sophisticated finishes. HPL uses a grooving system with rivets for installation, providing superior strength and a premium aesthetic. ACP is more budget-friendly for commercial use, while HPL offers luxury finishes for high-end projects.
How is HPL installed with grooving system and rivets?
HPL panels are installed using a grooving system with rivets. This involves creating grooves or channels in the HPL panels, which are then secured using aluminium rivets to the supporting framework. This method provides superior strength to withstand wind loads, creates a clean aesthetic with minimal visible fasteners, ensures long-term durability, and delivers a premium finish ideal for luxury projects. The grooving system allows for secure mechanical fastening that lasts for decades.
Should I choose ACP or HPL for my project?
Choose ACP if you have a commercial project (offices, malls, hotels, showrooms) where cost-effectiveness and quick installation are priorities. ACP uses screws and sealant installation, making it faster and more economical. Choose HPL if you have a luxury residential project or villa where premium aesthetics and sophisticated finishes are important. HPL offers natural wood textures and uses grooving system with rivets for superior strength and premium appearance. Our team can help you decide based on your specific project requirements and budget.
