Duct Shaft Louvers & Aluminium Louver Canopy · Design, Price & Technical Details

WoodenMax Duct Shaft Louvers & Aluminium Louver Canopy is one engineered 50x50 aluminium louver system that does two jobs — duct shaft screening / AHU louvers that hide MEP risers and machine openings, and entry canopy / gate canopy / facade sunshades that define a building’s character. Heavy-duty 50x50x1.5mm 6063-T6 profiles at 8-inch gaps deliver exceptional airflow + shade and mesmerising shadow patterns on the elevation.

Engineering Meets Aesthetics

Each canopy is precision-engineered using 6063-T6 grade aluminium with structural calculations for wind load resistance. The cantilever and floating frame designs create a dramatic visual impact while the angled louver blades optimize sun shading based on your building's orientation. Available in 12ft and 16ft profile sections with minimal wastage.

Versatile Applications

Perfect for entry gate canopies, terrace roof extensions, balcony sunshades, window facades, and car porch covers. The louver canopy reduces direct solar heat by up to 40% while maintaining natural ventilation—a smart solution for India's tropical climate. 🏛️ Transform your building entrance into an architectural landmark.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the profile size for aluminium louver canopy?
We use 50x50x1.5MM 6063-T6 grade aluminium profiles. These heavy-duty square profiles are installed every 8 inches (200mm) gap to provide optimal shade coverage while allowing air circulation.
Can the canopy withstand heavy wind and rain?
Yes, absolutely. Each canopy is designed with structural engineering calculations for safety and durability. The heavy-duty 50x50x1.5MM aluminium profiles and robust mounting system are engineered to withstand wind loads, rain, and harsh weather conditions.
Why is aluminium better than wood or steel canopy?
Aluminium offers unmatched advantages: no rust like steel, no termites or rot like wood, no repainting required, and no structural bending over time. The zero-maintenance benefit makes aluminium the most cost-effective long-term solution.
How much heat reduction does louver canopy provide?
The louver canopy reduces direct solar heat gain by 30-40% while maintaining natural ventilation. The angled blades block harsh sunlight and create shade while allowing air to flow through.
Can the canopy design be customized?
Fully customizable. We can adjust louver angle, blade spacing, projection length, overall shape, and mounting style to match your architectural vision. Choose from cantilever, floating frame, or angular mount installations.
Can I use this calculator if I live outside India?
Yes. The live calculator on this page runs in your browser with no login—enter sizes in feet, metres, inches, cm, or mm. Supply, site visits, and final quotes remain primarily for projects in India—contact us with your country for export or special cases.
