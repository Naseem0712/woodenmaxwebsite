Frequently Asked Questions

Can we customize shape, size and spacing?
Yes. Everything is made as per architect's drawing & site design. We can custom-cut, bend, curve, profile, and space the louvers exactly according to your project requirements. Profile sizes can range from 50mm to 300mm, and spacing can be adjusted to achieve the desired aesthetic and functional goals.
Is it suitable for outdoor weather?
100% rust-free & UV-proof. Powder coat lasts 15–25 years. The premium-grade 6063 T6 aluminium combined with high-performance coatings (powder-coated, PVDF, or anodized) ensures excellent weather resistance. These louvers are designed for exterior use and can withstand harsh weather conditions including rain, sun, wind, and temperature variations without degradation.
Does it block wind or natural light?
It controls sun heat + adds privacy without blocking ventilation. By adjusting the blade angle and spacing, you can control the amount of sunlight and heat entering the building while maintaining airflow. The design allows natural ventilation and light control, making it perfect for sun-shading applications without compromising on air circulation.
Can it match wooden or metallic textures?
Yes. Multiple realistic finishes (wooden, sandstone, copper, matte). We offer a wide range of finish options including wooden texture, sandstone texture, metallic finishes like copper or bronze, and various matte colors. These finishes are designed to mimic natural materials while providing the durability and low maintenance of aluminium.
What is the price range for custom curved architectural louvers in Hyderabad, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune, Jaipur?
Price varies based on design complexity (flat/aerofoil/custom curved), profile size, finish option (textured/wood/metallic), coating type (powder/PVDF/anodized), and project size.
