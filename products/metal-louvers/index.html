Frequently asked questions

What is the aluminium louver price per sqft?

Standard fixed ventilation from ₹450/sqft; premium motorized up to ₹1,450/sqft including fabrication and install in metros.

What are louvers vs rafters?

Louvers — closer angled blades for sun/privacy. Rafters — deeper rectangular sections for ceiling and elevation rhythm. Same 6063-T6 alloy.

Which page has the live BOQ calculator?

Four calculator pages at the top of this hub — wooden finish, curved, ceiling pergola and canopy/duct. Each prices aluminium, coating and hardware from your sizes.
