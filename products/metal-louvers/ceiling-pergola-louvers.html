Frequently Asked Questions

Will it protect from rain?
Light rain protection is possible; fully waterproof option available with premium pergola system. The louver design allows controlled water flow, and with additional rain protection options, you can achieve better coverage during heavy rainfall.
Can we add lighting or fan with this pergola?
Yes, ceiling lighting, hanging lights, fan brackets & automation can be added. The pergola structure is designed to support integrated lighting systems, ceiling fans, and even automated controls for a complete luxury outdoor experience.
Which color is best for outdoor louvers?
Matte black, wooden finish, sand grey & texture brown are trending. These colors provide excellent UV resistance, blend well with outdoor environments, and maintain their appearance for years.
How long does the coating last outdoors?
Powder coating lasts 15–25 years, no rust, no fading. The premium powder-coated finish is specifically designed for outdoor use, providing excellent protection against UV rays, weather elements, and corrosion.
What is the price range for aluminium pergola ceiling louvers?
