FAQs

What is a slimline aluminium window?
A slimline aluminium window is a premium casement (side swing / top hung) with a thin profile for maximum glass area and a clean modern look. This page focuses on high-end series such as S35, S38, and 14 mm class systems with strong height and DGU options.
What height can slimline / high-end series reach?
S35 and S38 typically suit openings up to about 10 ft. 14 mm / S40-class high-end lines can reach about 14 ft height on suitable projects. Always confirm the exact opening with a site measure.
What glass and DGU options work with slimline high-end series?
Single toughened glass commonly 5–12 mm. Mid high-end series such as S35 / S38 commonly take thicker DGU packs (about up to ~30 mm class) for better sound and comfort. 14 mm / S40-class high-end lines commonly take DGU up to about ~34 mm and high-safety / laminated glass on suitable projects. Exact glass depends on the profile pocket and project brief.
Can I add mosquito mesh to a slimline casement?
Yes. This casement supports optional inside openable mesh (and optional 12 mm aluminium grills). Use the mesh checkbox in the live calculator or choose a package that includes mesh.
How much does a slimline aluminium window cost in India?
Rates start from about ₹900 per sq.ft before GST for base single-glass configurations. Typical planning band is ₹900–1,400/sqft depending on glass, mesh, grill, and lock upgrades. Multipoint lock is ₹3,000 per window here. Use the live calculator for your exact size.
Which aluminium brands and finishes are used?
Profiles commonly use 6063-T6 alloy from brands such as Vitoc, Hindalco, and Jindal. Colour: powder coating with unlimited colour options including wooden powder coating; anodizing with limited colours. Exterior-grade powder brands include AkzoNobel and Zotun. Finish options: smooth colour, textured colour, wooden finish.
