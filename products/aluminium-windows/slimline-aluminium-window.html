Slimline Aluminium Window ( Slim Profile Window ) — Features, Materials & Specifications

The Slimline Aluminium Window uses thinner profiles than standard windows, giving you more glass area and a cleaner look. The frame is still strong enough to support large glass panels—typically 5-12mm single glass or 16-22mm DGU for better insulation. This design works well for modern homes where you want maximum natural light without bulky frames taking up space.

Premium Materials

This window features Hindalco or Imported aluminium profiles - both options offer superior strength and durability. The imported profiles allow for custom powder coating in any RAL color, while Hindalco profiles provide excellent value with standard finishes.

Saint-Gobain Glass

We exclusively use Saint-Gobain glass in all configurations. Choose from single glass (5-12mm) for standard applications, Double Glazed Unit DGU (16-22mm) for enhanced thermal and sound insulation, or laminated glass for superior safety and soundproofing.

Imported Hardware

All hardware components are 100% imported, ensuring smooth operation, superior security with multi-point locking, and long-lasting performance. The imported hardware requires minimal maintenance and provides years of trouble-free operation.

25mm Slim System — Luxury Look, Economical Budget

The 25mm slim system series is the smartest upgrade from basic Domal windows. Here is what actually changes:

What you get: Proper interlock depth and genuine system hardware — water leakage and sound leakage drop dramatically compared to Domal. With a good powder coating finish, this series gives a truly luxury feel in a budget price range. Glass options: 5–12mm, laminated, and DGU up to 18mm.

Honest limitation: On large sizes (8–9 ft) and in high-rise buildings, vibration is possible in this series. If your opening is large or you live on a high floor, step up to the 29mm system series — that is exactly the problem it solves.

Who should choose this: Apartments and homes that want the slim premium look and sealed performance without paying for the heavier series. For standard bedroom and living room sizes on low and mid floors, this series is excellent value.

FAQs

What is the difference between Hindalco and Imported profiles?
Hindalco profiles are premium Indian-made aluminium profiles, excellent quality and cost-effective. Imported profiles offer superior design flexibility and custom powder coating options in any RAL color. Both are high-quality; choose based on your design requirements and budget.
Which glass type should I choose?
Single glass (5-12mm) is ideal for standard applications. DGU glass (16-22mm) provides better thermal and sound insulation — recommended for bedrooms and areas near noise. Laminated glass offers enhanced safety and superior soundproofing for high-noise areas.
Can I get custom colors?
Yes, custom powder coating is available on imported profiles in any RAL color. Standard Hindalco profiles come in anodized silver and standard colors.
Is the 25mm slim system window good for high-rise buildings?
For standard sizes on low and mid floors, yes. On large openings (8–9 ft) and high floors, vibration is possible in 25mm — choose the 29mm system series there.
What glass can a 25mm slim system window take?
5–12mm single glass, laminated glass, and DGU up to 18mm total thickness.
