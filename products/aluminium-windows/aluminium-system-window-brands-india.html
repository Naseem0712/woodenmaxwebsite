FAQs

How do I compare aluminium system window brands fairly?

Ask for the same glass build-up, wind load, and hardware tier on every quote — then compare type-test references and install methodology, not just ₹/sqft.

Are imported systems always better?

Not always — many Indian OEM lines meet performance if installation follows published details. Import often wins on hardware feel and long-span engineering.

Can WoodenMax work with a brand my architect already specified?

In most cases, yes — share PDF details so we can partner with the right fabricator and validate anchorage for your site.
