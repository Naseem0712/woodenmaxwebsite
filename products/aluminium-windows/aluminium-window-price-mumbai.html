Why Mumbai projects need a different spec

Mumbai has a specific climate, wind and exposure profile that changes the right product choice. Here is what we recommend per condition, and why.

  • Climate — coastal, high humidity, very heavy monsoon. Salt exposure mandates Qualicoat Class 2 Seaside powder coating.
  • Wind zone — Moderate (Zone 3); design face-load of 1.8 kPa (~155 km/h gust) qualified per IS 4351 / EN 12211 on our systems.
  • Smart picks for Mumbai — Qualicoat Class-2 Seaside coating mandatory (salt-spray rated) · Class-9A water tightness on high-rises 12+ floors · Acoustic DGU (STC 35+) on western-railway-facing buildings.
Coastal cities: skip the value tier
In Mumbai, the salt + humidity combination eats a budget-tier powder coat in 3–4 years. We recommend skipping the value tier and starting at mid-range with Class-2 Seaside coating. The 18–22% premium pays back in 7 years vs. one re-coat cycle.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest aluminium window I can get in Mumbai?

Entry-tier 2-track sliding with Indian hardware and single-glazed clear glass starts at ₹550/sqft (basic). For a typical 6-sqft bedroom window that's about ₹3300. Add 18% GST. Transport is free if your order qualifies (≥ ₹15 L).

Is the price for a Mumbai project different from Hyderabad?

Basic aluminium window price is the same nationwide because manufacturing happens at our Hyderabad factory. Mumbai-specific variations are only in (a) transport, (b) any spec upgrade like Class-2 Seaside coating for coastal exposure, (c) install logistics like crane charges for high-rises.
