Coastal vs inland guidance — Seaside protection

Sea-facing and near-coast towers should specify Qualicoat Class 2 Seaside (salt-spray rated) powder coating. Budget inland-grade coats corrode faster in Mumbai’s salt + humidity mix — upgrade the coating rather than inventing a new ₹/sqft city rate.

More inland / sheltered pockets (some Powai / Thane contexts) may still use standard Class 2 if the survey confirms low salt exposure — but monsoon water-tightness remains non-negotiable on windward elevations.

Coastal Mumbai: start mid-range with Seaside coating
For salt-exposed elevations we recommend skipping a bare Value single-glaze package and starting at mid-range with Class-2 Seaside coating plus proper drainage / gasket detailing. Exact coating and hardware are confirmed on site survey.

Frequently asked questions

What is the aluminium window price range in Mumbai?

WoodenMax publishes ₹550–₹2250/sqft nationally (basic, before GST). Mid-range with DGU is often around ₹1150/sqft. Mumbai does not use a separate invented rate card — coastal coating and access equipment are specification add-ons confirmed after survey. Use the calculator for opening-wise totals.

Do Mumbai windows need Seaside / salt-spray coating?

On sea-facing and high salt-exposure elevations, yes — specify Qualicoat Class 2 Seaside. Sheltered inland-facing openings may use standard Class 2 if the survey confirms low salt load. Coating choice does not replace water-tightness detailing for monsoon.

What matters for high-rise water-tightness in Mumbai?

Wind-driven rain on taller towers needs proper drainage paths, gasket quality and hardware that maintains seal under pressure. Exact class targets are set after opening orientation and floor height are surveyed — not guessed from a city template.

Should railway-facing Mumbai flats use acoustic DGU?

Yes for bedrooms and living rooms that face Western Railway or other heavy corridors. Acoustic / laminated DGU sits inside the same published price bands once glass is selected. See the soundproof windows guide.

Are hoist or crane charges included in the ₹/sqft?

No. Basic ₹/sqft covers supply of the specified window system. High-rise hoist, scaffold or crane — when the building cannot move crates via lift — is quoted at actuals after site survey per the installation policy.

Why is Mumbai aluminium window ₹/sqft the same as Hyderabad?

Because manufacturing is at the Hyderabad factory — basic aluminium window ₹/sqft is national. Mumbai deltas are Seaside coating where needed, acoustic glass, transport eligibility and install logistics (access/hoist), not a parallel city price list.

How do GST and free transport work for Mumbai?

GST @ 18% is always extra on basic value. Mumbai is 711 km from Hyderabad — within the 1,000 km free-transport radius on qualifying orders (≥ ₹15 L). Details: GST & transport policy. Payment on confirmed orders: 20% token, 70% before dispatch, 10% on snag-free handover.
