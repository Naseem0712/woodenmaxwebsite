Where Openable Doors & Casement Windows Fit Best

Hinged 50 mm and 65 mm openable doors and side-hung casements suit bedrooms, entrances, and villa vents that need a full shutter seal — better dust and weather control than a sliding panel in those openings.

  • Single-leaf hinged door — typical bedroom / study entrances (about 3×7 or 3×8 ft) on 50 mm.
  • Double-leaf French door — living / patio openings (about 6×8 ft) on 50 mm or 65 mm.
  • Side-hung casement — matching windows with compression seal beside hinged doors.
  • Heavy-duty entrance — taller hinged doors on 65 mm when site engineering allows.

Full 40 mm top-hung / soft-vent window → dedicated 40 mm page. Slimline S35 / S38 / 14 → slimline aluminium window. Wide balcony panels → aluminium sliding window.

Stile glass door lines stay distinct from generic 50 mm openable cards — see Slim Entrance when that slim-stile product applies.
