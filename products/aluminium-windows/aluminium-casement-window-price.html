Casement vs Sliding — When to Switch Pages

Stay on this casement calculator if…

Opening is small (bath, kitchen, stair), you need full-frame vent, and swing space is clear. Casement seals better on dust and sound at the same size.

Switch to sliding if…

Balcony, living room, or any wall wider than ~6 ft where half-panel sliding beats multiple small casement vents. Start at sliding vs casement guide or 2-track sliding.

Price comparison (honest)

Budget Domal sliding can undercut casement on ₹/sqft — but on small wet-area vents, casement hardware cost is often worth the sealing gain. Compare both calculators with the same glass spec.

Best Rooms for Casement & Top-Hung Windows

  • Bathrooms — top-hung stays open in light rain; compression seal keeps dust out. Most common use of this calculator.
  • Kitchen service windows — vent above platform without a shutter swinging into the work area.
  • Staircase & utility vents — high openings where directed airflow matters more than clear width.
  • Bedroom side vents (small) — when a full 3-track slider is overkill; pair with sliding on the main balcony wall instead.

Not ideal for: wide balcony walls, floor-to-ceiling living glass — use system sliding series on those openings.

Casement & Openable Series Guide — 40mm to 72mm

Casement (openable) systems have fewer profile series than sliding — but each one is engineered for a specific job. The full range: 40, 45, 50, 52, 65 and 72mm. Top hung, side swing, uplift doors and style doors are all built from this same section family, and so are entrance doors and French doors.

Why choose casement over sliding at all?

  • Maximum opening space — the full frame opens, not just half. Perfect where the window itself is small: bathrooms, kitchens, staircase windows.
  • Better sealing — the shutter presses shut against EPDM gaskets, controlling dust and sound to the maximum level.
  • Better security — multi-point safety locks are far stronger than sliding latches, which is why entrance and French doors are built from this family.

Honest disadvantages:

  • The shutter needs clear swing space — inside or outside. In tight rooms or near furniture, a casement fights for space that sliding doesn't need.
  • Shutter width is limited (3 ft in 40–45mm; 3.5–4 ft even in 65–72mm) — wide openings need multiple shutters or a sliding system instead.
  • Hinges carry the full shutter weight every single open/close — quality hinges are non-negotiable, and heavy shutters on cheap hinges sag within a couple of years.

FAQs

Which casement window series is best for a normal flat?
40–45mm. Builders and apartments use it widely — heights of 6–7 ft are easy, with DGU up to 22mm. Keep each shutter within 3 ft width, because this series is lightweight with low depth.
Can I use DGU glass in 65mm or 72mm casement doors?
No. The 65–72mm profiles take only single glass (5–12mm) or laminated glass up to 13.52mm. For DGU, choose the 50–52mm series, which accepts up to 38mm glass.
What is aluminium casement window price per sqft?
Our top-hung outward opening casement series commonly prices between ₹750 and ₹1050 per sq.ft depending on mesh, grills, glass upgrades, and multipoint hardware.
Casement vs sliding — which costs more?
For similar glass, casement hardware (friction stay, multipoint) can match or exceed simple sliding rollers. Use both calculators to compare for your sizes.
Can I add mesh to casement windows?
Yes — inside openable mesh is popular for insect protection without losing the outward vent pattern.
