Sliding vs Casement Window — Price, Ventilation & Which to Pick (2026)

Indian homes rarely need only sliding or only casement — balconies and living rooms want sliding; bathrooms, kitchens, and stair vents want top-hung casement. This page compares cost, sealing, maintenance, and room fit with a 29mm sliding calculator below plus a link to the casement price tool.

Quick answer: sliding wins on wide openings and zero swing space; casement wins on small wet-area vents, dust/sound sealing, and entrance doors. Compare both with the same glass spec before you decide on ₹/sqft alone.

Which Window Type for Each Room?

Balcony & living room — sliding

No swing into furniture; half-panel vent is enough for most balconies. Wide walls need 3-track (glass + mesh) or system sliding — start at 3-track sliding or system sliding price.

Bathroom, kitchen, staircase — casement / top hung

Small openings where the full sash should open and compression sealing beats brush seals. Use the casement calculator for 40mm top-hung pricing.

Entrance & French doors — casement family only

Multipoint locks and EPDM compression are non-negotiable on main doors. See slim entrance door or French Georgian product pages.

Can I mix both in one home?

Yes — that is normal good design. Sliding for large openings, casement for small and wet areas. The detailed comparison table is in the next section.

The straight answer:

  • Big openings, balconies, living rooms → Sliding. Nothing matches it for wide glass.
  • Small windows where every inch of opening matters (bathroom, kitchen, staircase) → Casement/top hung. The full frame opens and the compression seal beats sliding on dust and sound.
  • Entrance & French doors → Casement family, always — the lock systems and sealing are simply in another class.
  • Both in one home is normal — most well-planned homes use sliding for large openings and casement for small/wet areas. That's not a compromise; that's correct design.

FAQs

Which seals better against dust and sound — sliding or casement?
Casement. The shutter compresses against EPDM gaskets when closed, controlling dust and sound to the maximum level. Sliding windows seal with brushes and interlocks, which are good in system series but cannot match compression sealing.
Should I use sliding or casement windows in my home?
Both, in the right places. Sliding for large openings (balconies, living rooms), casement/top hung for small openings (bathrooms, kitchens, staircases) and all entrance doors. This is how well-planned homes are designed.
Sliding vs casement — which is cheaper in India?
Budget Domal sliding can be among the lowest ₹/sqft options. Premium sliding aligns closer to casement. Compare both calculators with the same glass spec.
Which is better for balcony?
Sliding is most common for balconies because it does not swing into furniture. Casement can work if external space is clear.
Which seals better in heavy rain?
Quality casement with multi-point compression can seal extremely well. Sliding relies on brushes and drainage — both work when detailed correctly.
