3-Track Sliding Window — Which Option and Why?

A 3-track window gives you glass + glass + mosquito mesh sliding together — the most practical configuration for many Indian homes. This page is for WoodenMax 3-track aluminium sliding with mesh (optional 2-track without mesh on the same calculator).

This 3-track line: Practical for bedrooms and balconies up to about 6 ft height. Rates start from ₹850/sqft with mesh; 2-track without mesh from ₹650/sqft. Glass, lock, and coating upgrades are separate.

Premium 29mm / system sliding: Better when you need sealed joints, high-rise stability, or heavier glass. See aluminium sliding window and system sliding.

Why Choose a 3-Track Sliding Window?

In simple words: Three tracks let you slide glass + glass + mosquito mesh together — practical everyday ventilation with insect protection.

Best for: Bedrooms, balconies, and ground-floor rooms where you want air without insects. Homes that need sealed joints and high-rise stability should step up to 29mm system sliding.

When to skip 3-track: Large living openings where you only want glass and maximum view — a 2-track or 4-track wide opening may fit better.

FAQs

What is a 3-track aluminium sliding window with mesh?
A 3-track sliding window has two glass panels plus one sliding mosquito mesh on a third track. You can ventilate with insect protection without removing the mesh. This WoodenMax page is for that product — rates start from ₹850/sqft for 3-track with mesh; optional 2-track without mesh from ₹650/sqft on the same calculator.
What is the difference between 2-track and 3-track sliding windows?
2-track = two glass panels, no mesh — maximum glass area and a clean look. 3-track = two glass panels plus one sliding mesh panel — ventilation with insect protection. We recommend 3-track for bedrooms and balconies; 2-track for large living-room windows.
How much does a 3-track sliding window cost per square foot?
Typical projects start from ₹850/sqft for 3-track with mesh (2-track without mesh from ₹650/sqft; glass, lock, and coating extras separate). Final price depends on size, track option, and glass selection. Use our price calculator for an instant estimate for any size.
What is the difference between 2-track and 3-track sliding window price?
On this calculator: 2-track without mesh starts at ₹650/sqft; 3-track with mesh starts at ₹850/sqft. Glass, lock, and powder coating upgrades are separate. Use the calculator to compare prices for your specific size.
Can mesh be added later, or should I order it upfront?
On this page, choose 3-track with mesh (from ₹850/sqft) or 2-track without mesh (from ₹650/sqft). Mesh is built into the 3-track option — it is not a separate add-on line. Order the track configuration you need upfront.
What is the maximum safe height for this 3-track sliding window?
We recommend a maximum height of 6 ft for stable operation. You can go to 6–8 ft, but some vibration may occur. For taller openings or high-rise sealed performance, choose a premium 29mm series or a system window.
Which aluminium sliding window series is best for a high-rise flat?
29mm system series and above. From 29mm onwards there is no vibration even in high-rise buildings, and a complete hardware ecosystem (wheels, rails, seals) keeps operation smooth for years. Below 29mm, vibration is possible on large sizes.
What is the golden rule for sliding window sizes?
Keep each sliding shutter's width at least half of its height. This keeps the wheels balanced and the sliding operation soft and effortless for the life of the window.
How many powder-coating colours are available?
Standard colours — white, off-white, black, grey, and brown — are included. Wooden grain finishes (teak, rosewood, oak look) cost about ₹50/sqft extra. Custom RAL colours are available on imported profiles. Black and grey are the most popular choices in modern homes today.
How long do aluminium sliding windows last?
6063-T6 aluminium frames typically last 20–25 years. Powder coating lasts 10–15 years. Imported rollers and hardware stay smooth for 8–10 years. Annual maintenance is limited to track cleaning and roller lubrication — no painting and no rust.
What is DGU glass and when should I choose it?
DGU (Double Glazed Unit) has a sealed air or argon gap between two panes. It can cut heat transfer by up to 40% and reduce noise by around 35dB. Best for AC rooms, road-facing windows, and premium villas. Extra cost: roughly ₹180–220/sqft. Choose DGU if your electricity bill is high or traffic noise is a problem.
What is the difference between aluminium sliding windows and uPVC windows?
Aluminium is about three times stronger, allows slimmer frames, and does not warp in Indian heat. uPVC offers slightly better insulation but can sag on large openings. Budget guide: this 3-track aluminium line from ₹850/sqft vs uPVC ₹700–1,100/sqft. For most Indian climates, aluminium is the better long-term choice.
