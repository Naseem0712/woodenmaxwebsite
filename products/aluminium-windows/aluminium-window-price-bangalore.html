Standard vs acoustic / DGU — and 2-track vs 3-track

Single glaze suits quieter interior-facing openings on a Value budget. DGU is the usual mid-range upgrade for thermal comfort and everyday traffic noise. Acoustic / laminated DGU is for bedrooms and living rooms that face ORR, flyovers or airport-corridor traffic — still within the same published ₹/sqft bands once glass and hardware are selected.

2-track is the default apartment slider. Choose 3-track when you want a dedicated mesh leaf without removing a glass panel. Neither option invents a Bengaluru-only price — calculator line items stay national.

Bengaluru is inland — standard coating is enough
Standard Qualicoat Class 2 powder coating is sufficient for Bengaluru’s temperate inland climate. Marine / salt-air coating packages belong on exposed seafront cities — not Bengaluru apartments or villas.

Frequently asked questions

What is the aluminium window price range in Bengaluru?

WoodenMax publishes ₹550–₹2250/sqft nationally (basic, before GST). Entry 2-track starts at ₹550/sqft; mid-range with DGU is often around ₹1150/sqft. Bengaluru does not get a separate invented rate card — use the calculator for opening-wise totals.

2-track or 3-track for a Bengaluru apartment?

Choose 2-track for a simple slider. Choose 3-track when you want a dedicated insect-mesh leaf that can stay on while glass panels slide. Price still follows the same national bands once profile, glass and hardware are selected.

When should Bengaluru homes use acoustic DGU?

Prioritise acoustic / laminated DGU on bedrooms and living rooms that face ORR, flyovers or airport-corridor traffic. Quiet courtyard-facing openings can often stay on standard DGU or single glaze within the Value/Mid bands. See the soundproof windows guide.

Do Bengaluru villas need different systems than apartments?

Often yes on span and hardware, not on a special city price. Villa decks and larger Whitefield/Sarjapur openings may need lift-and-slide or heavier sliding/casement sections; apartments more often stay on 2-track or 3-track. Quote from measured openings.

Is a marine / salt-air coating required in Bengaluru?

No. Bengaluru is inland and temperate. Standard Qualicoat Class 2 is the usual coating for local apartments and villas.

Does Bengaluru get a different aluminium window ₹/sqft than Hyderabad?

No — manufacturing is at the Hyderabad factory, so basic aluminium window ₹/sqft is national. Bengaluru quote deltas are transport eligibility, glass/hardware upgrades (e.g. acoustic DGU) and site access — not a parallel city price list.
