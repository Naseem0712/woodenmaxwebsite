Sliding vs Casement — Quick Routing Guide

Which is cheaper on ₹/sqft?

Budget Domal 3-track can undercut premium 29mm 2-track — compare both calculators. Casement on small wet-area vents often matches sliding on total project cost when sealing gain is factored in.

Which is better for home?

Balcony sliders win for clear opening without swing-space conflict. Casement wins on bathrooms and kitchens under 3 ft shutter width. Most well-planned homes use both — sliding on large walls, casement on small vents.

Maintenance

Vacuum tracks quarterly, check drain holes after monsoon. Casement hinges need less frequent alignment than roller paths but still need annual lubrication on coastal projects.
