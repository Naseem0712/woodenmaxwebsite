Series Selection for Homeowners — Budget vs Premium

Rental / tight budget — Domal 3-track

Lowest ₹/sqft path for bedrooms that need mesh. Heights under ~6 ft, moderate wind exposure. Calculator: 3-track Domal · Domal pricing.

Owner-occupied flat — 29mm premium sliding

Better rollers, sealing, and finish for daily living-room and balcony use. This page’s calculator uses this series as the reference band.

Long-term / high-rise — system 30–35mm

When floor height, wind load, or DGU weight rules out Domal. Essential for tower balconies used every day — system sliding price.

Design-led facade — slim minimal

Narrow sightlines for villas and premium apartments — higher ₹/sqft, engineer review on glass weight. See slim luxury.

What Drives Total Home Window Cost

Opening sizes matter more than “brand”

A small bathroom casement and a full-wall living slider sit on different ₹/sqft curves — sum each opening from its calculator instead of averaging one rate across the flat.

Glass by exposure

Road-facing bedrooms → DGU or laminated; internal baths → 6mm single. Full adder list: glass breakdown.

Hardware & security

Ground-floor sliders: multipoint locks or laminated glass. Entrance doors always casement-family with multipoint — never budget sliding latches on a main door.

Installation & monsoon readiness

Proper sill drainage and silicone detail matter in Indian monsoons — included in standard install bands; mention floor level and external cladding when you request quote.

Sliding-Only vs Mixed Home — What We Recommend

Sliding everywhere?

Cheaper on paper for small wet-area vents — but casement seals better on bathrooms and kitchens where the opening is narrow and you want full-frame ventilation.

Casement everywhere?

Poor fit for wide balconies — shutter width limits and swing clearance make sliding the correct choice for living walls.

The hybrid plan (recommended)

Sliding for large openings, casement for small and wet areas, system casement for entrance — this is how most well-planned Indian homes are built, not a compromise.

Who This Home Guide Is For

  • First-time homeowners choosing windows during interior fit-out — before carpenter blocking is cast.
  • Joint family upgrades — different rooms, different priorities (quiet bedroom vs open living).
  • WFH / study rooms — prioritize DGU or laminated on the road-facing lite.
  • Builders selling flats — document a clear spec per room instead of one generic “aluminium window” line.

Why We Recommend Sliding for Living & Bedroom (Not Every Room)

Simple answer: Indian homes need wide glass + mesh + daily sliding — 3-track handles that in bedrooms; living rooms prefer 2-track or 4-track for view.

Best budget path: Domal 3-track in rental bedrooms; owner-occupied flats step to system 3-track on the same layout.

FAQs

Which aluminium window is best for living room?
Premium 29mm sliding or slim combinations for views; add DGU if you face traffic noise.
Which window is best for Indian kitchens?
Top-hung casement or sliding with easy-clean tracks — casement helps vent smoke if external space allows.
What about safety?
Pair ground-floor sliders with grills or laminated glass; casement multipoint helps security.
