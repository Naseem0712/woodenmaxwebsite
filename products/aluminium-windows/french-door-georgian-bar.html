Luxury Aluminium French Door — Features, Materials & Specifications

A luxury aluminium french door is the go-to choice for balcony openings, living room exits and villa entrance doors when you want slim frames and maximum glass. Made from 6063 T6 aluminium, this french door has multiple openable panels at the bottom and a fixed decorative panel at the top — an openable glass door that looks elegant while maintaining security and weather resistance. This premium aluminium french door is popular in luxury villas and premium apartments across India. Optional: add a golden Georgian bar pattern for a classic European accent (₹80-120/sqft extra depending on pattern complexity).

Premium 35mm Slim Profile Series

This luxury french door features a 35mm slim profile series, which is a premium specification offering superior strength and aesthetics. The slim profile provides a modern, elegant look while maintaining structural integrity — ideal for tall balcony french door openings and premium entrance doors. Note: Optional Georgian bar styling is not included in the base rate.

8mm Toughened Glass Included

The base rate includes 8mm clear toughened glass, providing excellent clarity and safety. You can upgrade to 10mm, 12mm, DGU (Double Glazed Unit), or Safety glass for enhanced performance. When you select 12mm, DGU, or Safety glass, the hardware automatically upgrades to premium multipoint hardware to support the heavier glass.

Maximum Height 9 Feet

This door is designed with a maximum height of 9 feet, ensuring optimal structural stability and performance. The height limitation ensures that the door maintains its integrity and smooth operation while providing ample clearance for most residential and commercial applications.

Premium Materials & Security

Made from 6063 T6 high-grade aluminium, this door offers exceptional strength and durability. Combined with premium toughened glass, standard hardware, and optional multipoint or mortice lock systems, it provides maximum security for your home entrance. Optional security mesh is also available for added protection.

French Doors & Georgian Bars — Where Luxury Lives

French doors on this page use the casement section family (40–72mm) — multi-point safety locks and compression EPDM soundproofing come built in. For entrances and balconies, that means a door that locks like a vault and seals like a window.

French sliding option: Same Georgian look on a 2-track slider for wide balcony openings — see French sliding window if you need stack-side operation without swing space.

Georgian bars — the honest truth

Functionally, Georgian bars do almost nothing. We'll say it plainly. But two things happen when they go on:

  1. The space instantly gets that classic luxury feel — entrance doors, kitchens and balcony doors of premium homes.
  2. They genuinely strengthen the glass by dividing and supporting the pane.

Luxury Aluminium French Door — Frequently Asked Questions

Do Georgian bars have any functional benefit?
Honestly, very little — their job is the classic luxury look. The one real benefit: they strengthen the glass by dividing and supporting the pane. Cost is roughly ₹3,000–15,000 per door depending on design complexity.
Why are French doors more secure than normal doors?
They are built from the casement section family, which carries multi-point safety locks and compression EPDM sealing — better security and soundproofing than ordinary door systems.
What is the 35mm slim profile series used in this luxury aluminium french door?
The 35mm slim profile series is a premium specification offering superior strength and aesthetics. Designed for luxury applications, it provides a modern, elegant look while maintaining structural integrity and durability.
What glass is included in the base rate for this aluminium french door?
Base rate includes 8mm clear toughened glass. You can upgrade to 10mm, 12mm, DGU, or Safety glass. Selecting 12mm, DGU, or Safety glass automatically upgrades hardware to premium multipoint for proper support.
What is the maximum height available for this luxury french door?
Maximum height is 9 feet for optimal structural stability and performance. The calculator will alert you if you exceed this limit.
How does the hardware cost change when I select different glass types?
Standard hardware is included. Selecting 12mm, DGU, or Safety glass automatically upgrades to premium multipoint hardware for proper support. The calculator applies this upgrade automatically.
What lock options are available for this aluminium french door?
Options: Single Point Lock (included), Multipoint Lock, or Mortice Lock (upgrades). Multipoint provides enhanced security by locking at multiple points along the door frame.
