FAQs

How much does DGU glass add versus 6mm toughened?

DGU upgrades air gap insulation and usually adds a higher ₹/sqft delta than single toughened — exact adders appear in the calculator dropdown for premium sliding.

When is laminated glass worth it?

Safety, security, and acoustic goals — especially ground floor or nursery rooms.

Does thicker glass need stronger hardware?

Often yes — sash weight rises; multipoint or heavy-duty rollers may be required.
