Types & Design Options

2 track vs 3 track vs 4 track

2 track is usually two glass panels. 3 track adds a mesh or third panel path. 4 track supports wider openings with more panels — expect higher aluminium window cost with glass because of extra profiles and rollers.

Sliding vs casement

Sliding saves space inside; casement swings for maximum vent areas in kitchens and baths. See sliding vs casement comparison.

Slim vs standard

Slim sightlines cost more per kg of profile and need precise glass specs. Standard 29mm series balances price and durability.

Price Breakdown (Material, Glass, Size)

Frame cost

Hindalco or imported 6063 T6 profiles, powder coating, and reinforcement for wind load.

Glass cost

6mm toughened is typical; DGU and laminated add ₹/sqft for acoustic and thermal goals.

Hardware cost

Locks, rollers, and end caps — multi-point upgrades add per-window charges.

Installation cost

Façade level, silicone, fasteners, and labour — final quote after site check.

Comparison (vs Other Options)

Which is cheaper?

27mm Domal 3-track systems often beat premium 29mm 2-track on ₹/sqft but differ on glass stack and hardware feel.

Which is better for home?

Living and balcony: sliding. Bathrooms and utility: casement or top-hung.

Maintenance

Clean tracks quarterly; casement hinges need lubrication less often than roller paths.

Best Use Cases (Home / Office / Villa)

  • Apartment living rooms: 2 track sliding for noise and view.
  • Offices: DGU for HVAC efficiency.
  • Villas: combine with elevation systems.

FAQs

What is the 2 track aluminium window price per sqft in India in 2026?

Premium 29mm series 2 track aluminium sliding windows typically fall between ₹1200 and ₹1400 per sq.ft installed, before GST, depending on glass upgrades, coating, mesh, and hardware. Budget 27mm Domal systems can start lower — see our 3 track page for Domal-style pricing.

Is a 2 track window cheaper than a 3 track or 4 track sliding window?

More tracks usually mean more aluminium, hardware, and machining — so multi-track systems often cost more per opening. 2 track is efficient for simple glass + glass sliding. 3–4 track adds mesh or extra panels for wider spans.
