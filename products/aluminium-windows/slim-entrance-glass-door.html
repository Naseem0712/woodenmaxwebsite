FAQs — S40 Slim Sliding / Slim Entrance

What is the S40 slim sliding / slim entrance glass door line?
It is WoodenMax’s high-end S40 luxury slim sliding line presented on the slim entrance glass door page — thin 40mm-class frames, 14 mm interlock on luxury meets, and thicker DGU options for villa-grade openings. It is not a bathroom vent product.
What height can S40 / 14 mm high-end reach?
S40 / 14 mm high-end lines can reach about 14 ft height on suitable projects. Always confirm the exact opening with a site measure. The on-page door calculator also applies its configured max-height check for package/door sizing.
What glass and DGU options work with S40?
Base includes 8mm clear toughened. Calculator upgrades cover 10mm, 12mm, DGU, and safety glass. S40 high-end lines commonly take thicker DGU packs (about up to ~34 mm class) and high-safety / laminated glass on suitable projects. Exact glass depends on the profile pocket and project brief.
What interlock do slim and luxury lines use?
Most slim and luxury lines use a 14 mm interlock for a cleaner sightline and a more premium meet of the shutters. Slim interlock is included in the base rate here.
How much does S40 slim sliding / slim entrance cost?
Rates start from about ₹1860 per sq.ft before GST. Glass upgrades and lock upgrades are priced separately in the live calculator so they are not double-counted. Use the calculator for your exact size.
