What “Domal” means here

  • Domal = 27×65 (27mm) budget profile — light frame, lowest common rate band from ₹650/sqft (2-track) · ₹850/sqft (3-track).
  • Inside Domal, pick a track: 2-track without mesh (more glass) or 3-track with mesh (2 glass + 1 mesh shutter). One calculator — switch track in the form.
  • Stay on Domal when: rentals, builder flats, low floors, openings under ~6 ft height, budget first. Choose 29mm premium when: high-rise, DGU/noise, long seal life → 29mm 2-track sliding.

This page prices the 27mm Domal aluminium sliding series (27×65mm, ~1–1.2mm wall). It shares the site’s 3track-sliding calculator and packages — not a second product identity. Prefer a mesh-first landing for the same series? See also 3-track sliding window (same rate source).

Room / use examples

2-track vs 3-track (both Domal)

Domal Window — Honest Fabricator's Guide

Domal here means the 27×65 (27mm) budget aluminium sliding series — not the premium 29mm page. Inside Domal you still choose 2-track (no mesh) or 3-track (with mesh) on the same calculator.

Why it sells: Lowest common rate band, light frame, mesh option available, profiles easy to source.

Honest limits: Basic connectors and shallow depth — water and sound leakage are common. Max recommended height 6 ft. Later, usually only wheels are replaceable.

When Domal is right: Rentals, builder projects, low floors, openings under 6 ft, budget-first scopes.

When to step up: Slightly better seal/look on a still-economical budget → 25mm slim system. High-rise, taller openings, DGU → 29mm premium 2-track or 29mm system sliding.

FAQs

What is a Domal window?
The 27mm aluminium profile series, popularly known as 27x65. It is India's most common budget window — lowest cost, lightweight, mesh possible — but with basic connectors and shallow depth, water and sound leakage are common and maximum recommended height is 6 ft.
Is a Domal window good for high-rise apartments?
Not ideal. For high-rise use, the 29mm system series and above have zero vibration and properly sealed joints. Domal works best on low floors with openings under 6 ft height.
Can Domal windows be repaired?
Only the wheels can be replaced. Track, seals and other components cannot be serviced — this is the biggest long-term difference versus system windows, where every component is individually replaceable.
What is the difference between aluminium sliding windows and uPVC windows?
Aluminium is about three times stronger, allows slimmer frames, and does not warp in Indian heat. uPVC offers slightly better insulation but can sag on large openings. Budget guide: 27mm Domal aluminium from ₹650/sqft (2-track) · ₹850/sqft (3-track) vs uPVC ₹700–1,100/sqft. For most Indian climates, aluminium is the better long-term choice.
What is the difference between 2-track and 3-track sliding windows?
2-track = two glass panels, no mesh — maximum glass area and a clean look. 3-track = two glass panels plus one sliding mesh panel — ventilation with insect protection. We recommend 3-track for bedrooms and balconies; 2-track for large living-room windows.
What is the difference between 27mm Domal and 29mm premium series?
27mm Domal: 1–1.2mm wall thickness, from ₹650/sqft (2-track) · ₹850/sqft (3-track), max recommended height 6ft — ideal for standard homes. 29mm Premium: uniform 1.2mm wall thickness, slim interlock, ₹1,200–1,400/sqft, up to 80kg panel capacity — built for premium residences.
What is DGU glass and when should I choose it?
DGU (Double Glazed Unit) has a sealed air or argon gap between two panes. It can cut heat transfer by up to 40% and reduce noise by around 35dB. Best for AC rooms, road-facing windows, and premium villas. Extra cost: roughly ₹180–220/sqft. Choose DGU if your electricity bill is high or traffic noise is a problem.
How long do aluminium sliding windows last?
6063-T6 aluminium frames typically last 20–25 years. Powder coating lasts 10–15 years. Imported rollers and hardware stay smooth for 8–10 years. Annual maintenance is limited to track cleaning and roller lubrication — no painting and no rust.
What is the maximum safe height for a sliding window?
For 27mm Domal, we recommend a maximum height of 6ft. You can go to 6–8ft, but some vibration may occur. The 29mm premium series stays stable up to 8ft. For openings above 8ft, choose a premium series or a system window.
Can mesh be added later, or should I order it upfront?
On a 3-track window, mesh is integrated — the track is built in from the start. Clip-on mesh can be added later but will not slide as smoothly. If you need mesh, specify it at order time.
How many powder-coating colours are available?
Standard colours — white, off-white, black, grey, and brown — are included. Wooden grain finishes (teak, rosewood, oak look) cost about ₹50/sqft extra. Custom RAL colours are available on imported profiles. Black and grey are the most popular choices in modern homes today.
Which glass is best for 3 track sliding window?
For 27mm aluminium sliding windows, base includes 5mm clear tuff glass. You can upgrade to 6mm clear glass for better strength or 8mm clear glass for enhanced durability. For standard residential use, 5mm or 6mm glass works perfectly. Choose 8mm if you need extra strength for larger windows.
