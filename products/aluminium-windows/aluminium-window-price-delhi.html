Do Delhi NCR homes need thermal-break profiles?

Often yes on west/south-facing and large glazed openings given the summer-to-winter delta. Thermal-break + low-E DGU stays inside published soft bands once specified — confirmed on survey.

How does Delhi dust and PM 2.5 affect window choice?

Dust-tight EPDM gaskets and proper sill detailing reduce ingress. Coating remains standard Class 2 inland — dust seals matter more than marine coating here.
