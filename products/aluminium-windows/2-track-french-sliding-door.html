2 Track Aluminium French Sliding Door — Features, Materials & Specifications

The 2-track French sliding door combines elegance with practical design. Built from 6063 T6 grade aluminium profiles, this system uses a 2-track setup where the outer doors stay fixed while the inner pair slides smoothly. This configuration gives you a wide opening when needed—perfect for connecting indoor spaces to patios or balconies. Ideal for living rooms and modern entrances, it maximizes natural light while maintaining security.

Frame Material

We use 6063 T6 grade aluminium profiles, which are stronger than standard aluminium. This grade handles heavy glass panels better and resists warping in extreme weather. The profiles come in 1.2mm wall thickness, giving the frame enough strength to support large door panels without sagging over time.

Glass Type

The top arch panel uses fixed glass, typically 6mm or 8mm clear glass. You can upgrade to DGU (double glazed) for better insulation or safety glass for ground-floor installations. The sliding panels use the same glass options, with 6mm clear included in the base price.

  • 6mm clear glass — included in base price
  • 8mm/10mm/12mm — thicker options for larger openings
  • DGU (double glazed) — superior insulation and noise reduction
  • Laminated safety glass — recommended for ground-floor installations

Roller Capacity & Performance

The imported rollers handle up to 100kg per panel, ensuring smooth operation even with large glass doors. Weather gaskets create a tight seal between panels, reducing external noise by 30-35dB—ideal for homes near busy roads. Ergonomic handles provide comfortable grip and easy operation.

Warranty & Maintenance

  • Frame: 10-year warranty against manufacturing defects
  • Hardware: 5-year coverage on rollers and locks
  • Glass: 1-2 years (manufacturer dependent)
  • Powder coating: Resists fading for 5-7 years

We recommend annual maintenance: cleaning tracks and lubricating rollers to ensure smooth operation.

Safety & Standards

  • Meets IS 14846 standards for aluminium windows
  • Ground-floor installations: Use laminated safety glass + multi-point locks
  • Regular track cleaning prevents debris buildup
  • Ensure proper drainage in heavy rainfall areas

French Sliding vs Plain 2-Track — Quick Difference

Plain 2-track: Rectangular glass, lowest cost, any bedroom. French sliding: Arch or divided-light look, wider visual opening — spec'd for balcony-to-living and entrance bays, not bulk bedroom counts.

Same 2-track mechanics (glass slides on tracks). Premium is in profile shape, glass layout, and optional Georgian bars (+₹3k–7k/panel).

Why Choose a French Sliding Window?

In simple words: French sliding windows use an arch or divided-glass look on sliding tracks — wider, brighter openings with a premium feel. They slide like normal windows; they do not swing open like casement.

Best for: Balcony openings into the living room and entrance / lobby windows where you want a special look — not the default choice for every bedroom or utility window.

Georgian bar option: Aluminium Georgian bars on the glass add strength and a classic premium look. Simple bar design is roughly ₹3,000–3,500 extra per panel; complicated patterns can add ₹5,000–7,000 per panel. See Georgian bar French windows for details.

Not everyday mass product: If a client wants this look, we can do it — cost is higher than plain sliding. For standard rooms, a normal 2-track or 3-track window is usually enough.

FAQs

Which glass is best for sliding French doors?
For homes: 8–12mm toughened glass. For noise/weather control: DGU / Laminated glass provides excellent sound insulation and weather resistance.
What is 6063 T6 aluminium?
It is a high-strength architectural grade aluminium, used for luxury and long life windows/doors. The T6 temper provides superior strength and durability compared to standard aluminium grades.
Do aluminium sliding doors reduce noise?
Yes. With imported gaskets + laminated/DGU glass, it provides excellent sound resistance. The weather-proof gaskets create a tight seal that significantly reduces external noise.
What is the price range for 2 track French sliding doors?
Price varies based on size, glass type, and coating finish. Typically ranges from ₹1,850/sq.ft to ₹2,250/sq.ft. Use our calculator above for instant estimates or contact us for a detailed quote.
Which coating finish should I choose?
Choose based on your design preference: Texture finish for modern industrial look, Smooth matt for contemporary style, Wooden coating for traditional warmth, or Powder coated for custom colors matching your décor.

What is the difference between single point, multi-point, and mortice lock?
Single point lock (included) provides basic security. Multi-point lock (+₹1000/door) offers enhanced security with multiple locking points. Mortice lock (+₹1500/door) is the most secure option, ideal for main entrances.
