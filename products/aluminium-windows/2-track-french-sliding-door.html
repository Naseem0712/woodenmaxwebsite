S35 French Sliding Door — Design, Mesh & Glass

This page is for the S35 series French / 2-track French sliding door. The core identity is a 2-track 4-door arrangement: the two side panels are fixed and the two centre panels slide. The optional top fixed arch glass panel adds the French-door look while the sliding mechanism stays practical for balcony, living-room and entrance openings.

The approved planning rate is simple: ₹1450/sqft without mesh. Tick integrated mesh in the calculator only when needed; that adds ₹200/sqft, making the mesh path ₹1650/sqft. Glass upgrades, lock upgrades and coating upgrades remain separate, so the same cost is not counted twice.

S35 belongs to the mid sliding series family where S34/S35/S38 are typically used around the 10 ft height class on suitable projects. Mid-series DGU is commonly planned up to about 30 mm, subject to profile pocket, glass weight, shutter proportions and site conditions.
