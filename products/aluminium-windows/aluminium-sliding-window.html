Smooth Gliding Operation

Features imported hardware with precision-engineered rollers for effortless sliding. The window glides smoothly on high-quality tracks, ensuring years of trouble-free operation. For example, a 6ft × 4ft window opens and closes with minimal effort, even after years of use. The roller mechanism is designed to handle weights up to 80kg per panel without sagging or sticking.

Integrated Insect Mesh

Optional integrated bug mesh keeps insects out while allowing fresh air in. The mesh is seamlessly integrated into the frame design, maintaining the sleek appearance. The mesh uses fine-grade fiberglass or stainless steel wire (0.3mm thickness) that blocks mosquitoes, flies, and other insects while maintaining 70% air flow. Easy to remove and clean when needed.

Premium 29mm Series Specifications

The 29mm sliding series features robust construction with 1.2mm wall thickness for all sections including track and shutters, ensuring exceptional durability and structural integrity. The shutter sections are precisely engineered with 29X65mm dimensions, providing optimal strength and performance. This thickness prevents frame warping even in extreme weather conditions (temperatures from -5°C to 50°C).

This series incorporates a slim interlock mechanism positioned in the middle section, delivering a premium luxury feel and seamless operation. The slim interlock design not only enhances the aesthetic appeal but also ensures smooth sliding action with superior weather sealing, making it perfect for modern architectural applications. The interlock creates an airtight seal that reduces noise by up to 35dB and prevents water ingress during heavy rains.

Hardware & Material Options for Aluminium Sliding Window

  • Multi-Point Lock: Handle-type lock (+₹1,000/window). 3-5 point locking system for enhanced security, recommended for ground floor and high-security areas.

Safety Notes

  • Multi-point locks recommended for enhanced security

  • Professional installation required for proper weather sealing and operation

Which Sliding Window Series Should You Choose?

Every aluminium sliding window belongs to a profile series — and the series decides how it performs, not the brand name. Here is the honest fabricator's guide:

SeriesMax SizeDGU GlassBest ForHonest Limitation
27mm Domal (27x65)6 ft heightNot recommendedBudget homes, rentals, builder projectsWater & sound leakage common
25mm Slim SystemMedium sizesUp to 18mmLuxury look on economical budgetVibration on 8–9 ft+ and high-rise
29mm System8–9 ft widthUp to 24mmVillas, flats, balconies, high-riseWidth should be at least half of height
31/34/35mm SystemLarge openings26–28mmPremium villas, bedrooms, entrancesHigher cost
38/40mm Minimal12–14 ft height28mmLuxury villas, farmhousesHeavy, premium price

Quick decision:

  • Tight budget, small windows, rental property → 27mm Domal (accept the leakage trade-off)
  • Want a premium look without premium price → 25mm Slim System
  • High-rise flat or villa, want zero vibration and long smooth operation → 29mm System (our most recommended)
  • Want invisible joints and serious soundproofing → 31/34/35mm
  • Luxury home with huge glass openings → 38/40mm Minimal

One rule that applies to all sliding windows: keep the shutter width at least half of its height. Follow this and the sliding operation stays soft and effortless for years.

Why Choose a Premium 29mm Sliding Window?

In simple words: Panels slide left–right on tracks — no swing space inside the room. You get big glass, easy daily use, and a clean modern look.

Best for: Living room, bedroom, and balcony openings where you want maximum glass and smooth sliding every day. Works well in flats and villas when you pick the right series (29mm and above for high-rise).

Not the first choice when: The opening is very small (like a bathroom vent) — a small openable window is easier there. Need sliding mesh on its own track? Look at a 3-track window instead of basic 2-track.

FAQs

Which aluminium sliding window series is best for a high-rise flat?
29mm system series and above. From 29mm onwards there is no vibration even in high-rise buildings, and a complete hardware ecosystem (wheels, rails, seals) keeps operation smooth for years. Below 29mm, vibration is possible on large sizes.
What is the golden rule for sliding window sizes?
Keep each sliding shutter's width at least half of its height. This keeps the wheels balanced and the sliding operation soft and effortless for the life of the window.
What is an aluminium sliding window?
An aluminium sliding window is a modern window system where panels slide horizontally on tracks. These windows are made from premium aluminium profiles (29mm series) with 1.2mm wall thickness, featuring smooth gliding operation, integrated mesh options, and premium hardware. Aluminium sliding windows are ideal for modern homes, apartments, and commercial spaces due to their durability, weather resistance, low maintenance, and sleek design. They provide excellent ventilation while maintaining security and aesthetics.
