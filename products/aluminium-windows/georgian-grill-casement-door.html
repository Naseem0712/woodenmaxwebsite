Georgian Grill Doors — Design, Cost & Where They Belong

A Georgian grill casement door pairs the casement family's engineering (locks, EPDM seals, dust & sound control at maximum level) with the most recognisable luxury detail in door design.

Where they belong:

Entrance doors, kitchen doors, balcony doors — the places guests see first and homeowners touch daily. The profiles are sleek and premium; the Georgian pattern adds the classic character.

What it costs: ₹3,000–15,000 extra per door for the Georgian work, depending on design and complexity. The door itself follows series pricing — 40–45mm for budget builds (heights 6–7 ft, shutter width 3 ft), 50–52mm for premium homes (up to 10 ft, DGU up to 38mm).

Honest note: The bars are aesthetic-first. Their real functional contribution is added glass strength. If someone sells Georgian bars as a security feature, that's the locks doing the work — which come from the casement system, not the bars.

Luxury Aluminium Casement Door — Frequently Asked Questions

How much do Georgian bars add to a door's cost?
Approximately ₹3,000–15,000 per door depending on the design and its complication. Simple grid patterns cost less; intricate custom patterns cost more.
Can this aluminium door be used as a main door?
Yes, with laminated/toughened glass + multipoint locking + grill combination. The strong 6063 T6 aluminium profile and multipoint locking system provide the security needed for main entrance doors. Combined with laminated or toughened glass, it offers excellent protection for your home.
Does the Georgian grill block visibility?
No. It adds luxury design without reducing visibility or light. The Georgian grill pattern is decorative and enhances the door's aesthetic appeal while maintaining full visibility and natural light flow through the glass panels.
Can we add mesh with this door?
Yes. A separate sliding mesh door or fixed mosquito system can be added. This allows you to enjoy ventilation without insects, making it perfect for balconies, kitchens, and utility areas where mosquito protection is needed.
Which color suits this design best?
Champagne, Black, Wooden Texture & Rose Gold are most popular. These colors complement the golden Georgian grill pattern beautifully, creating a luxurious and elegant appearance. Choose based on your interior or exterior design theme.
