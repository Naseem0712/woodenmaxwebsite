Glass shower partition price per square foot

In most Indian cities you will hear wide quotes for the same opening. That is normal: glass shower partition rates move with toughened grade, hardware brand, door type (fixed, hinged, sliding), and how difficult the wall and floor are to seal.

As a planning band — not a substitute for site measurement — many projects land roughly in three tiers: entry-level quotes often cluster around ₹380–₹600/sqft when glass and fittings are simpler; mid projects with better hinges or channels frequently sit near ₹700–₹900/sqft; premium frameless-style layouts with thicker glass and imported hardware can cross ₹1,200–₹2,000+/sqft depending on size and city. Always ask whether the rate includes installation and silicone work.

  • Clarify if the quote is glass-only, hardware-only, or a full supply-and-fix package.
  • Corner and L-shaped runs add cuts, brackets, and sealing time — budget a little extra.
  • For layout comparisons before you buy, skim our curtain vs glass partition notes.

How glass thickness changes cost and safety

6mm toughened

Sometimes proposed for very small fixed panels with a lot of support. It is not the default for door leaves or large free spans; confirm with your supplier against Indian safety practice for showers.

8mm toughened

The workhorse for many homes — reasonable strength, easier handling, and a balance of cost and clarity. Pair it with quality hinges or sliding gear if the panel is wide.

10mm toughened

Common on premium frameless shower glass runs where the glass itself provides stiffness. Expect higher glass cost and heavier-duty hardware.

Thickness interacts with price in two ways: material weight and the grade of clamps, rollers, or tracks required. Our dedicated shower glass thickness guide goes deeper if you are shortlisting specs before vendors visit.

What else moves the final quote

Wall flatness, tile hardness, and whether you need core drilling or patch-up after removing an old door all change labour hours. Tall ceilings, odd angles, or stone-clad walls can slow cutting and bracket placement. Mention drain location and curb height early — installers hate surprises once silicone is on order.

Finish choice (clear vs frosted vs tinted) also nudges price; frosted or coated surfaces may carry a premium. Tie that decision to privacy and cleaning habits using bathroom shower glass types.

Installation cost you should plan for

Labour-only charges for a straightforward retrofit often fall in a modest band; complex enclosures, multiple panels, or sites that need extra alignment can cost more. We maintain a separate shower installation cost explainer with indicative numbers and what teams usually include.

Professional installation matters for water tightness and hinge alignment — especially on frameless doors where glass edge symmetry shows every flaw.

Maintenance: keeping glass clear for years

Daily habits beat harsh chemicals. A quick squeegee or soft wipe after hot showers reduces mineral buildup in hard-water areas. For sliders, keep the lower track free of hair and grit so rollers do not grind.

Our shower glass maintenance guide collects practical routines without brand hype.

Is a glass shower partition worth it?

For many households, yes — you get better splash control than an open walk-in, a cleaner look than plastic curtains, and a longer service life when hardware is serviced occasionally. The upfront cost is higher than fabric, but the total cost of ownership often favours glass if you plan to stay in the home for several years.

Start with honest measurements, pick thickness and finish with safety in mind, then use the calculator and two to three comparable quotes to sanity-check your market.

FAQs

What is a fair glass shower partition price per sqft in India?

Market quotes vary by city and specification. Many supply-and-fix packages fall in wide bands from roughly ₹380–900/sqft for common setups, with premium frameless-style work higher. Always confirm glass thickness, hardware brand, and whether installation is included.

Does 10mm glass always cost more than 8mm?

Usually yes — thicker glass weighs more and often needs stronger fittings. The jump is not only material; hardware and labour may rise with panel size and door type.

Is a glass shower partition waterproof?

With correct slope, quality gaskets or seals, and skilled silicone work, it should contain normal shower spray. Site conditions and user splash patterns still matter.

How long does installation take?

A simple fixed panel may finish in part of a day; full enclosures or sliding systems can take longer, especially if tiles need patching or old structures must be removed.

Should I choose clear or frosted glass?

Clear glass keeps the bath visually open; frosted or textured glass adds privacy. Cost and cleaning behaviour differ slightly — see our glass types guide for trade-offs.
