Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Regular Slim and Ultra Slim profiles?
Regular Slim profiles are 16x45mm – a perfect balance of strength and aesthetics. Ultra Slim profiles are 16x35mm – the most minimalist look with slimmer visible frames. Both are imported aluminium with the same quality finish and corrosion resistance.
Why towel handle instead of knob?
Towel handles offer dual functionality – you can hang a towel on them while also using them to open/close the door. They provide a better grip, especially with wet hands, and look more elegant than basic round knobs. It's a practical upgrade that enhances both usability and aesthetics.
What hardware finishes are available?
Available in Matt Black, Gold, and Mill Finish. Matt Black is the most popular for modern bathrooms. Gold adds luxury appeal. Mill Finish provides a classic chrome-like appearance. All finishes are applied using high-quality coating processes for durability.
Is this shower glass rust-proof?
Yes, the imported aluminium profiles are anodized and powder-coated for maximum corrosion resistance. This dual-layer protection ensures the profiles maintain their appearance for years, even in high-humidity bathroom environments. The hardware is also corrosion-resistant.
Is sliding option available?
This product is available in Openable (Hinged) configuration only. For sliding shower doors, please check our Slim Frame Shower Partition or Frameless Shower which offer sliding options.
