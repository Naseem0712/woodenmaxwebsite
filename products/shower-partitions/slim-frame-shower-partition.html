What is Fluted Glass?

Fluted glass features vertical ridges that create a beautiful textured effect. Light passes through, but details are obscured – providing privacy without blocking natural light. Also known as reeded glass, it's the trending choice for luxury bathrooms, hotels, and spas.

PVD Coated Gold Profile

The gold finish uses premium PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) coating technology. This ensures the gold remains vibrant, tarnish-free, and rust-proof for years – even in high-humidity bathroom environments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is fluted glass and why choose it?
Fluted glass (also called reeded glass) features vertical ridges that create beautiful texture. It provides privacy by obscuring details while allowing light to pass through. It's the trending choice for luxury bathrooms, adding elegance without blocking natural light.
Does the gold finish fade or tarnish?
No. The imported gold profile uses premium PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) coating technology. Unlike paint or electroplating, PVD coating is bonded at molecular level, making it tarnish-free, scratch-resistant, and rust-proof. It maintains its vibrant gold appearance for years, even in humid bathroom environments.
Which glass color should I choose?
Choose based on your bathroom theme: Clear for maximum light and openness with gold frame as the star. Grey for modern minimalist bathrooms. Black for bold contemporary contrast. Brown for warm, traditional aesthetics that complement the gold perfectly.
Openable or sliding – which is better?
Openable (hinged) doors swing open, ideal for spacious bathrooms with room for the door arc. Sliding doors glide horizontally, perfect for compact spaces as they don't require swing space. Both feature soft-close mechanism for smooth, quiet operation. Choose based on your bathroom layout.
What's the difference between Gold and Rose Gold?
Gold PVD has a classic yellow-gold appearance, while Rose Gold has a warmer pinkish tint. Both use the same durable PVD coating technology. Rose Gold is slightly premium priced and pairs beautifully with copper fixtures and warm interior tones. Choose based on your bathroom's color scheme.
