Entrance & parking canopy — technical brief

Application: drop-off porches, basement ramp covers, surface parking bays, and terrace mechanical roofs where a lighter pergola grid is insufficient. Supply is split into primary steel or aluminium structure and roof infill from our common glass / polycarbonate schedule — identical ₹/sqft lines to the rest of this catalogue so tender comparisons stay clean.

Steel (iron) option: high section modulus per cost for long single spans; finish is typically primer + PU / metallised system on site when fully welded. The calculator flags where factory powder is not applied to welded nodes. Aluminium option: corrosion-resistant shell with integrated powder coating; preferred when the canopy face aligns with shopfront windows and entrance metalwork.

Roofing: laminated or toughened glass over drives and walks per fallout rules; large polycarbonate roofs for budget car parks. Gutters, downpipes, and slab falls are coordinated at measurement. Coordination with glass elevation, railings, louvers, and ACP / HPL façades is done on a single elevation set.

BOQ lines follow calculator fields: structure ₹/sqft, roof ₹/sqft, coating, civil / anchor allowance — no bundled “lump sum” unless requested.

FAQ — steel vs aluminium, welding & coatings

Tap a question to expand or collapse the answer.

When is iron preferred over aluminium?

Long spans, car parks, and entrance porches often use iron for stiffness and cost; aluminium stays rust-free with powder coat when budget allows.

Parking canopies — what should we check first?

Column spacing for car sweep, turning radius, and gutter fall; we set downpipe positions during measurement.

How does on-site welding affect coating in the calculator?

Welded joints may skip powder coating in the tool; a separate site paint system can be quoted for exposure.

Are roof glass rates the same as other pergola pages?

Yes — toughened, laminated, and polycarbonate use the same published ₹/sqft bands.

Can we mix iron columns with an aluminium beam grid?

Hybrid schemes are possible; loads and connections are engineered per project.

What maintenance does an iron canopy need?

Periodic inspection of paint film and fasteners; touch-up per exposure class.

How fast can you install after order?

Lead time depends on fabrication load and glass lead; we give a schedule after drawing approval.
