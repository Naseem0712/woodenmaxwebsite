FAQ — glass roof pergola price India

What is glass roof pergola price per sqft in India?

WoodenMax publishes a planning band of roughly ₹1,300–2,400 per sq ft for fixed aluminium terrace pergolas with toughened, laminated or polycarbonate roof — ex-GST. Use our live calculator for your exact width × depth.

Does WoodenMax manufacture pergolas in-house?

Yes — we cut 6063-T6 aluminium profiles, powder-coat in controlled booths and install with dedicated outdoor-structure crews from our Hyderabad factory. Site teams cover Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Bangalore and pan-India projects.

Is laminated glass better than toughened glass for pergola roofs?

Laminated glass (6+6 mm) is recommended where people walk underneath or where safety codes require retained glass fragments. Toughened 10–12 mm clear glass is common on terraces with good slope and drainage.

How does glass roof pergola price compare to motorized retractable pergola?

Fixed glass-roof pergolas typically plan around ₹1,300–2,400/sqft. Motorized retractable systems start higher (~₹1,800–3,500+/sqft) — see our retractable pergola page.

Are published ₹/sqft rates final?

They are schedule rates for planning. Final numbers depend on span, wind load, hardware, coating and site access after survey.
