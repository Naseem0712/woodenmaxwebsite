Glass skylight & automatic roof — product scope

Fixed skylights deliver constant daylight to stair cores, corridors, and double-height spaces. The build-up is typically aluminium or steel perimeter framing, insulated kerb where required, and a glazed infill selected for span and fallout: laminated (two lites + interlayer) for walk-off zones, or toughened where the structural engineer approves support and net catchment below. Polycarbonate (solid / multiwall) is specified when weight or cost dominates; UV-side orientation and replacement interval are called out at tender.

Automatic sliding / folding roofs use the same ₹/sqft glass and polycarbonate lines as fixed pergolas for the closed position; incremental scope is motor, rack, guide, and seal package — brand- and span-specific — quoted after approved GA. Waterproofing follows system supplier limits: minimum pitch, end-stops, and overflow we review against your terrace drain layout.

Detail: weep paths, condensation control, and interface with windows and curtain wall are resolved on shop drawings. Façade continuity with HPL / ACP and edge protection via glass railing is scheduled in the same package where WoodenMax supplies those trades.

FAQ — overhead safety, automation & pricing

Tap a question to expand or collapse the answer.

Which glass is safest for an overhead skylight?

Laminated glass is the usual choice where people walk below; toughened options follow our rate table with engineer approval on span.

How do you reduce condensation?

We specify slope, weep holes, and edge details suited to your climate; ventilation strategy is reviewed on site.

Can automatic roofs use the same calculator?

Structure and roof infill use the same engine; motors and automation are quoted after layout and brand selection.

Is polycarbonate suitable for a stairwell top light?

It is lighter and economical; for premium clarity and long life many clients choose toughened or laminated glass.

What warranty applies to motors on sliding roofs?

Drive units follow the manufacturer’s warranty; glazing and metalwork follow WoodenMax project terms.

Do you measure on site before production?

Yes — openings, structural supports, and waterproofing interfaces are verified before glass order.

Are rates the same as other pergola product pages?

Yes — structure and roof ₹/sqft bands match our published schedule and the live calculator.
