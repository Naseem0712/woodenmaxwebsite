Glass-roof aluminium pergola — system description

This range is a factory-profiled aluminium space frame (primary members in 6063-T5 / T6) designed for terrace, pool deck, and garden overlays. Connections are mechanical — cleats, brackets, and stainless fasteners — so level and alignment can be corrected at installation. The roof plane is a separate specification: monolithic toughened (10 mm or 12 mm) for maximum clarity, laminated safety glass (PVB / SGP interlayer) where the panel is overhead to circulation, or solid / multiwall polycarbonate where budget or weight governs. Each option maps to the ₹/sqft lines in the tool above; structure is always priced on plan area of the pergola footprint.

Surface treatment: architectural powder coating to exterior grade — RAL solids, metallic, and wood-effect films on aluminium. Coating selection drives both aesthetics and coastal durability; we match adjacent louver and window colours where those packages are from WoodenMax.

Alternative structure: for long-span parking or entry canopies, the same roof glass and polycarbonate rates can be carried on a system iron or welded steel frame — switch structure type in the calculator; coating rules for welded steel follow the tool (site paint may apply).

Envelope integration: specify together with glass elevation, HPL / ACP cladding, and glass railing so slab edge, gutter, and head-of-wall details are drawn once. After you run the calculator, a site survey confirms wind speed, fixing substrate, and drainage — schedule rates are then adjusted only for verified span and access.

FAQ — aluminium pergola price, glass & installation

Tap a question to expand or collapse the answer.

Are motors included in the aluminium pergola price?

This page covers fixed glass-roof pergolas: pricing is structure + roof + coating. Motorized systems are priced on our retractable pergola line.

Why is a retractable roof more expensive than fixed glass?

Tracks, motors, seals, and reinforced members add scope beyond the roof glass schedule.

How is waterproofing handled in pergola roofs?

We design slope, gutters, and edge flashings for your slab; drainage is confirmed at survey.

Can polycarbonate sheets be used in pergolas?

Yes — solid and multiwall polycarbonate are selectable in the calculator with published ₹/sqft.

What is the typical installation lead time?

After drawing and glass approval, lead times follow fabrication and roof sheet procurement; we advise on your BOQ.

What roof options work on an aluminium pergola?

10 mm or 12 mm toughened clear glass, laminated safety glass (PVB/SGP), and solid or hollow polycarbonate — all priced from the same schedule as the calculator.

Is laminated glass mandatory overhead?

Not always, but we strongly recommend it where people walk underneath; laminated glass holds together if broken.

Can the frame match a wood look?

Yes — wooden-finish powder coating on aluminium is a standard pergola finish and pairs well with timber decks and HPL facades.

How do I get a full itemised quote after using the calculator?

Submit the short inquiry form in the tool; you get the line-by-line breakdown on screen and our team receives the same specification.

Do you supply and install outside Hyderabad?

We work across major Indian cities; remote sites are scheduled with regional partners where needed.

What is the difference between system fitting and on-site welding?

System fitting uses factory profiles and nut-bolt assembly; welding suits heavy iron frames and may change coating rules — select the mode in the calculator.

Are published ₹/sqft rates final?

They are schedule rates; final numbers depend on span, wind load, hardware, and site access after survey.
