Motorised retractable pergola — scope of supply

This product line covers motor-operated roof systems on an aluminium or steel primary frame: linear sliding glass, stacking / folding panels, or retractable fabric (brand-dependent). The online estimate isolates what is standard across our pergola range — structural frame ₹/sqft and roof glazing or polycarbonate ₹/sqft — so you can compare directly with a fixed glass-roof pergola. Automation (motor, controller, travel rails, weather seals, safety edge) is itemised in the formal BOQ once span, speed, and IP rating are fixed.

Structural difference vs fixed roof: members are sized for track pull-out, point loads at carriers, and repeated cycling; eaves and ridge profiles are deeper. Coastal and tall buildings may require upgraded fasteners and finishes — noted at survey.

Interfaces: drainage, overflow, and slab waterproofing are coordinated with the motor supplier’s limits. Visually, the system is often specified alongside glass façade, balustrades, sun louvers, and terrace doors; ACP / HPL soffits complete the outdoor room.

Optional wind / rain sensors and BMS tie-in quoted with electrical scope — they do not alter published glass or metal rates.

FAQ — motors, waterproofing & calculator scope

Tap a question to expand or collapse the answer.

Are motors included in the calculator total?

The on-screen estimate covers structure and roof glass or polycarbonate. Drive units, sensors, and rails are quoted after we confirm span and brand.

Why is a retractable roof more than a fixed glass roof?

Tracks, motors, seals, and reinforced eaves add cost beyond the glass schedule priced in the calculator.

How do you handle waterproofing?

Seals and slope follow the system supplier; we review drainage and overflow paths during site survey.

Same toughened and laminated rates as a fixed pergola?

Yes — roof infill uses the same ₹/sqft bands as our fixed-frame pergola line.

Can I use polycarbonate on a sliding roof?

Where span and profile allow, yes; final choice depends on wind load and manufacturer limits.

Typical lead time after measurement?

Profile and glass are ordered after sign-off; motorized systems may add lead time for drives and controls.

Do you service motors later?

Warranty and service follow the motor brand’s terms; we coordinate with authorised partners where needed.
