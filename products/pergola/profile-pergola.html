Profile louver pergola — system description

The system is a rectangular hollow-section grid in architectural aluminium, optimised for visual slimness and modular repetition on cafés, club decks, and residential sit-outs. Horizontal members carry louver blades or purlins for a solid roof; verticals tie to slab or grade with calculated base plates. Loads are taken for wind pressure and deflection limits per project location; purlin spacing is iterated against the selected roof glass or sheet.

Louver mode: blades may be fixed (manual tilt lock) or motorised with tubular or rack drives — motor schedule is separate from the structure + glass calculator. Roof mode: 10 / 12 mm toughened, laminated, or polycarbonate as per the same rate table as our standard pergola.

Finish: exterior powder — RAL / woodgrain / texture — matched where possible to façade louvers, windows, and HPL / ACP cladding on the same elevation. Deck edge often pairs with glass railing and glass wall lines for one metal-and-glass language.

FAQ — louvre layout, motors & rates

Tap a question to expand or collapse the answer.

What is a profile louver pergola?

A modular frame built from slim aluminium profiles; you can pair open louvers with a solid or glass roof using the same rate engine as our other pergola pages.

Can louvers be motorised?

Yes — motors and controls are quoted per brand, load, and span after layout approval.

What is the maximum span?

It depends on profile section, purlin spacing, and wind zone; we verify against relevant IS codes on each project.

Are ₹/sqft rates the same as the main aluminium pergola page?

Structure and roof use the same published bands; the calculator applies them to your footprint and selections.

Is polycarbonate a good choice for a café terrace?

It is cost-effective and light; for premium clarity and longevity many clients upgrade to toughened or laminated glass.

Do you provide site measurement?

Yes — book a survey through contact; we confirm levels, drainage, and fixing points.

How long does installation usually take?

Small residential units often complete in a few days; larger commercial spans depend on crane access and finishing scope.
