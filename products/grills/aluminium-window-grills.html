Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of aluminium window grills per sqft?

Aluminium window grill price ranges from ₹350 to ₹450 per sqft depending on profile size (12mm to 25mm), thickness (1.2mm to 2.0mm), and coating finish (plain, texture, or wooden grain).

Are aluminium window grills rust-proof?

Yes, aluminium window grills are 100% rust-proof. Unlike iron grills, aluminium naturally forms an oxide layer that prevents corrosion. With powder coating, they last 15+ years without any maintenance.

What is the best grill design for modern windows?

For modern windows, vertical pattern grills with 19x19mm or 25x25mm square profiles give the cleanest look. Round 12mm pipes create a minimal aesthetic. Pair with plain black or champagne gold powder coating for a contemporary finish.

How to calculate aluminium grill cost for my window size?

Use our free calculator above: enter width, height, and quantity. Select your preferred profile size, pipe shape (round/rect/oval), thickness, and coating finish. The calculator instantly shows the exact price including material, labour, installation, and wastage costs.

Which coating finish lasts longest on aluminium grills?

All three finishes (plain, texture, wooden grain) use premium Jotun or AkzoNobel powder baked at 200°C and last 15+ years. Texture finish offers better scratch resistance. Wooden grain finish gives a natural wood look without the maintenance of real wood.

Can aluminium grills be customized for any window size?

Yes, every grill is custom-fabricated to your exact window measurements. We support any width and height combination. You can also choose different pipe spacing patterns (uniform, alternating 2, alternating 3) and add horizontal dividers for larger openings.
