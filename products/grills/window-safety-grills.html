Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of window safety grills?

Window safety grill price ranges from ₹330 to ₹440 per sqft. These use thicker profiles (2.0mm or 2.2mm) and reinforced joints for maximum security.

What makes safety grills different from regular grills?

Window safety grills use thicker aluminium profiles (2.0-2.2mm vs standard 1.2mm), closer pipe spacing, and optional threaded iron rods for anti-cut protection.

Which grill thickness is best for ground floor security?

For ground floor windows, use minimum 2.0mm wall thickness with 25x25mm outer frame. Add 8mm or 10mm threaded iron rods for anti-cut protection. This combination makes it virtually impossible to cut through.

What is the difference between safety grill and decorative grill?

Safety grills use thicker pipes (2.0-2.2mm), closer spacing, and iron rod reinforcement. Decorative grills use thinner pipes (1.2mm) with wider gaps for aesthetics. For ground floor security, always choose safety grade.

Can window grills be removed for cleaning?

Standard grills are permanently fixed. For removable options, we offer grills with concealed bolt mounting that can be opened with a special key. This costs ₹50-80/sqft extra but allows window cleaning access.

How much weight can aluminium window grills bear?

Aluminium grills with 25x25mm frame and 2.0mm thickness can bear 80-100 kg of impact force. With 10mm iron rod reinforcement, this increases to 150+ kg. Sufficient for residential and commercial security.
