Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of iron grills per sqft?

Iron grill price ranges from ₹220 to ₹330 per sqft. They are the most budget-friendly option but require regular painting to prevent rust.

Iron grill vs aluminium grill — which is better?

Iron grills are cheaper initially but rust over time and need annual maintenance. Aluminium grills cost more upfront (₹385-495/sqft) but are rust-proof and maintenance-free for 15+ years.

Is iron grill cheaper than aluminium grill?

Yes, iron grills cost ₹220-330/sqft vs aluminium ₹385-660/sqft. However, iron requires painting every 2-3 years (₹30-50/sqft each time). Over 10 years, aluminium costs less due to zero maintenance.

How to prevent iron grills from rusting?

Apply anti-rust primer before painting. Use enamel paint, not water-based. Repaint every 2-3 years. Avoid direct water contact. For rust-free alternative, choose aluminium grills which need no maintenance.

What is MS grill and how is it different?

MS (Mild Steel) grill is another name for iron grill. MS grills use mild steel pipes and rods welded together. They are strong but heavy and prone to rust. Aluminium grills are lighter and rust-proof.

Can iron grills be powder coated like aluminium?

Yes, iron grills can be powder coated, but the coating doesn't last as long as on aluminium. Iron expands and contracts more with temperature changes, causing powder coat to chip faster. Aluminium powder coating lasts 15+ years.
