Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of staircase balustrade grills?

Staircase balustrade grill price ranges from ₹495 to ₹660 per sqft depending on profile size, design complexity, and coating finish.

Can aluminium balustrades support weight?

Yes, aluminium balustrades with 25x50mm profiles and 2.0mm thickness provide excellent structural support. They are anchored with SS 304 fasteners into concrete or masonry.

What is the standard height for staircase railing?

As per Indian building codes, the minimum staircase railing height is 900mm (3 feet). For buildings above 3 floors, 1050mm (3.5 feet) is recommended. Our balustrade profiles can be customized to any height.

Is aluminium balustrade strong enough for staircases?

Yes, aluminium 6063-T6 alloy balustrades with 25x50mm frame and 2.0mm thickness meet all structural requirements. They can withstand 100+ kg lateral force. For added strength, iron rod reinforcement is available.

What is the price difference between glass railing and aluminium balustrade?

Aluminium staircase balustrade costs ₹495-660/sqft. Glass staircase railing costs ₹1000-1500/sqft. Aluminium is more child-safe with no gaps, while glass offers a more modern transparent look.

Can aluminium balustrade be used for ramps?

Yes, aluminium balustrades are excellent for ramps, wheelchair ramps, and handicap-accessible areas. The continuous handrail design with no sharp edges meets accessibility standards. We custom fabricate for any ramp angle.
