Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of balcony safety grills?

Balcony safety grill price ranges from ₹440 to ₹605 per sqft depending on height, profile thickness, and coating. Full-height enclosures with thicker profiles cost more.

Are balcony grills safe for children?

Yes, our balcony safety grills are designed with narrow gaps (2-3 inches) that prevent children and pets from passing through while maintaining full ventilation.

What is the minimum gap size for child-safe balcony grills?

For child safety, the maximum gap between grill pipes should not exceed 4 inches (100mm). Our calculator lets you set custom gap sizes. We recommend 2-3 inch uniform gaps with 12mm round or 19x19mm square pipes for optimal child safety.

Can balcony grills be installed on high-rise apartments?

Yes, aluminium grills are ideal for high-rise balconies because they are lightweight (60% lighter than iron), rust-proof, and don't add excessive load to the building structure. We provide installation for buildings up to 30 floors.

What is the difference between balcony grills and balcony glass railings?

Balcony grills (₹440-605/sqft) use aluminium pipes for full security coverage. Glass railings (₹800-1200/sqft) use tempered glass panels with SS brackets for an unobstructed view. Grills are better for child safety; railings are better for aesthetics.

How long does balcony grill installation take?

Standard balcony grill installation takes 1-2 hours per panel. A typical 3-bedroom apartment with 3 balconies can be fully installed in one day. Custom designs with dividers may take slightly longer.
