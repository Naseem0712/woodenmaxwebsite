Frequently asked questions

Real buyer doubts — price bands, window type picks, glass, install timeline and calculator use.

What is the aluminium window rate per sq ft?

Typical installed bands in India (profile + glass + hardware + fitting):

3-track Domal sliding ₹550–950/sqft · Premium 2-track ₹1,200–1,400 · Top-hung casement ₹750–1,050 · French sliding door ₹1,850–2,250. GST 18% is extra.

The exact rate depends on opening size, glass tier, and finish — use the live calculator on any product card for an instant estimate.

For a bedroom or balcony, 3-track or 2-track sliding?

3-track = 2 glass panels + 1 mesh track. Ventilation with insect protection — the most practical choice for bedrooms, kitchens, and balconies.

2-track premium = more glass area and a cleaner look — better for living rooms and view-facing windows. Clip-on mesh can be added later but is not as smooth; decide at order time if you need mesh.

Sliding or casement — which window for which room?

Sliding: wide openings, balconies, living areas — smooth horizontal movement, minimal sill space.

Top-hung casement: bathrooms, kitchens, stairs — better sealing and full outward ventilation.

If road noise is an issue, consider casement + DGU or a system window. Compare both: sliding vs casement guide.

When should I choose DGU double glass?

Road-facing rooms, AC bedrooms, home offices — anywhere heat or traffic noise is a problem. DGU reduces heat transfer noticeably and improves noise control.

Typical adder ₹180–220/sqft on base toughened glass. Quiet internal rooms often work fine with 6–8mm toughened single glass. Details: glass price breakdown.

What is included in the quote?

Standard package: Hindalco/import profile, Saint-Gobain toughened glass, imported rollers & locks, shop fabrication, site installation, silicone sealing.

Separate line items: mesh track, multipoint lock upgrade, DGU/laminated glass, powder coat, arch panel. Tick options in the calculator to see the total — no hidden charges.

Why does the price change after a site measure?

Actual opening size vs drawing, wall level, lintel condition, floor access, and heavy-glass hardware requirements can all change the final hardware and labour cost.

A ±10–15% shift after site visit is common. Use the calculator for planning first; a firm quote follows a free site measure.

How long does installation take?

Typical flat (5–10 windows): 1–2 working days to install once frames are ready. Full villa elevation: 2–4 weeks, phase-wise.

Timeline is confirmed at order stage. Monsoon can slightly delay external sealing work — we flag this upfront.

What does the warranty cover?

10-year frame warranty on 6063-T6 profiles — manufacturing defects covered. Glass and hardware defects are covered under warranty terms.

First-year adjustment for roller smoothness and lock alignment is included. Accidental damage and third-party modifications are not covered.
