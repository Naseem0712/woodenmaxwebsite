Frequently Asked Questions

What types of aluminium windows do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range: 3-track sliding windows (with/without mesh), 2-track French sliding doors (with top fixed glass arch), top-hung casement windows (outward opening with multipoint lock), Georgian grill casement doors (with golden patterns), French doors with Georgian bar (35mm/40mm slim profiles), slim entrance glass doors (40mm super luxury), aluminium sliding windows, and full elevation villa facades.

Which aluminium profiles do you use?

We use premium Hindalco aluminium profiles (6063 T6 grade) for standard applications and imported profiles for custom designs and specialized projects. Both offer superior strength and durability. Hindalco is cost-effective while imported profiles offer design flexibility and custom powder coating options.

What type of glass do you use?

We exclusively use Saint-Gobain glass: Single glass (5-12mm) for standard applications, Double Glazed Unit DGU (16-22mm) for enhanced thermal and sound insulation, Laminated glass for safety and soundproofing, and Safety glass (13.52mm) for high-security applications.

Do you provide powder coating and color options?

Yes, premium powder coating is available on imported profiles in any RAL color. Ready stock colors include Dark Grey, Matt Black, and Champion Gold. Other RAL colors are available on order. Standard Hindalco profiles come with anodized silver and standard finishes.

What hardware do you use?

We use 100% imported hardware in all our aluminium windows - including hinges, handles, locks, rollers, and friction stays. Imported hardware ensures smooth operation, durability, and superior security. Premium multipoint hardware upgrades automatically for heavy glass options.

What is the price range in Hyderabad, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune?

Prices vary based on window type, profile, glass, and hardware. Domal 27mm sliding with Hindalco profiles start from ₹550/sq.ft. Premium 29mm 2-track sliding with imported profiles, powder coating, and DGU glass ranges ₹1200-1400/sq.ft. Luxury French doors and slim entrance glass doors range ₹1850-2250/sq.ft. Most product pages feature live price calculators for instant quotes. Contact us for free site visit and accurate pricing.

What warranty do you provide?

All our aluminium windows come with a comprehensive 10-year warranty covering profiles, glass, and hardware. The warranty covers manufacturing defects, material quality, and performance issues. We also provide free site visit and consultation to ensure proper installation and long-term satisfaction.

Can I use this calculator if I live outside India?

Yes—live calculators with ₹ pricing are open to everyone, with no login. Enter sizes in feet, metres, inches, cm, or mm. From outside India you may see a local-currency rough guide (India base + FX). Final supply and site visits focus most on Hyderabad, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Jaipur, Lucknow, and similar locations—contact us for export or special cases.

What can I use on WoodenMax without signing up?

You can run live price calculators on linked product pages, switch measurement units, add multiple sizes where the tool allows, and compare glass or finish options in real time. Use the quote or contact form only when you want a firm quote or call-back—no account needed for estimates.

Are specs in English for architects and NRIs?

Yes. Profiles, glass types, hardware, and finishes are documented in English so consultants and homeowners abroad can align with local codes. Hindi/India search intent and ₹ bands on this hub stay unchanged; the tools simply help you budget before talking to our team.
